# Trips Terms and Conditions {#trips-terms-and-conditions}

_Last Updated: 08/09/2026_

By purchasing a ticket and participating in an ESN Cyprus trip, you agree to the following Terms and Conditions. Please
read them carefully.

## 1. Ticketing, Resales & Eligibility {#ticketing-resales-eligibility}

1. **Age Requirement:** All participants must be at least 18 years of age to attend.

2. **ESNcard Policy:** An ESNcard is not mandatory to join the trip; however, an active ESNcard is required to access
   discounted presale ticketing phases.

3. **Refunds:** Participants are eligible for a full refund only if the cancellation request is submitted at least two
   weeks (14 days) prior to the event's start date. Exceptions may be considered in severe emergencies at the absolute
   discretion of ESN Cyprus.

4. **Ticket Resales & Transfers:** Tickets may be resold or transferred to another eligible student up to three (3) days
   before departure. You must officially notify ESN Cyprus of this transfer so that rooming and bus manifests can be
   updated. Any unofficial deviation, unapproved transfer, or resale outside of these terms will result in immediate
   ticket cancellation without a refund.

## 2. Transport, Safety & Health {#transport-safety-health}

1. **Departure Timings:** A strict 15-minute grace period applies to all scheduled bus departures. If you miss the
   departure, you are solely responsible for arranging and financing your own transportation to catch up with the group.

2. **Safety Car:** The ESN safety car is strictly reserved for medical emergencies, accessibility needs, and critical
   health-related situations. It is not available for latecomers or general transportation.

3. **Medical Declarations & Assistance:** Participants must explicitly declare any severe allergies, pre-existing
   medical conditions, or accessibility requirements during the ticket checkout process. Please note that ESN Cyprus
   volunteers
   are not medical professionals and cannot guarantee medical assistance beyond reasonable first-response measures and
   contacting emergency services. Participants are fully responsible for carrying any necessary medication.

## 3. Accommodation & Code of Conduct {#accommodation-and-code-of-conduct}

1. **Confidentiality & Security:** To ensure the safety and security of the group, all shared materials (especially
   accommodation locations and detailed itineraries) are strictly confidential. Sharing these details with
   non-participants is expressly prohibited.

2. **Venue Rules & Liability:** Participants must adhere to the accommodation curfews and quiet hours communicated prior
   to or during the trip. Individuals are fully financially and legally liable for any damages they intentionally or
   recklessly cause to their rooms, the transport vehicles, or the property.

3. **Behaviour & Removal:** ESN Cyprus aims to provide a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment. We reserve the
   right to immediately remove any participant for misconduct. Reasons for removal include, but are not limited to:

    * The possession or use of illegal substances.
    * Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or threatening behaviour.
    * Unwanted sexual behaviour or failure to respect another person's consent.
    * Physical aggression or violence.
    * Repeated refusal to follow instructions from ESN organizers, venue staff, or drivers.

Individuals removed from the trip will not receive a refund, and ESN Cyprus will not provide or cover the costs of their
return transportation.
