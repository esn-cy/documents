# Privacy Policy {#privacy-policy}

_Last Updated: 18/05/2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how ESN Membership Manager ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by ESN Cyprus, collects, uses,
shares, and protects your personal information when you use our platform to apply for, manage, and use your ESNcard and
ESN Cyprus Pass.

## 1. Information We Collect {#information-we-collect}

To provide you with our services, we collect the following personal information during the application process:

* **Identity Data:** Name, surname, date of birth, and nationality.

* **Contact Data:** Email address.

* **Academic & Mobility Data:** Host institution, ESN section, and mobility status (e.g., Erasmus Student, ESN
  Volunteer).

* **Media & Documents:** Scanned identity/mobility proof documents (in case of manual verification) and a face photo (in
  case of an ESNcard).

## 2. How We Use Your Information {#how-we-use-your-information}

We use your data strictly to:

* Verify your eligibility for the ESNcard or ESN Cyprus Pass.
* Issue and manage your membership, including physical ESNcards, digital ESN Cyprus Pass, and Guest Passes.
* Process payments for your membership.
* Prevent fraud and ensure compliance with ESN guidelines.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion (GDPR Compliance) {#data-retention-deletion}

We employ strict, automated data retention policies to ensure we do not hold your personal data longer than necessary.
Our system automatically processes data deletion according to the following schedule:

* **Identity Documents and Proofs:** Any uploaded ID scans and proofs of mobility are permanently deleted from our
  servers 14 days after your application is either approved or rejected.

* **Face Photos:** Face photos are permanently deleted 1 year (365 days) after your payment date (if you applied for an
  ESNcard).

* **Account Anonymization:** After 1 year, your personal database record is completely anonymised. Your name and surname
  are replaced with "Anonymised," your date of birth is reset to the 1st of January of your birthyear, and your email
  address is permanently obfuscated.

* **Associated Records:** At the 1-year mark, any Apple Wallet registrations, authentication codes, and guest passes
  tied to your account are permanently erased.

## 4. Third-Party Integrations and Data Sharing {#third-party-integrations-data-sharing}

To operate ESN Membership Manager effectively, we integrate with trusted third-party services. We only share the minimum
data required for these services to function:

* **ESN Accounts:** To verify your membership status automatically, your data is checked against the centralized ESN
  Accounts system managed by ESN AISBL in Belgium.

* **Stripe (Payments):** If your application includes and ESNcard, a Stripe payment link is added to your account. We
  share your application ID with Stripe to link your payment to your application. After one year, your active payment
  links are disabled.

* **Weeztix (Ticketing):** When you are assigned an ESNcard, your card number is securely transmitted to Weeztix to act
  as an active coupon code for event discounts.

* **Google Services:**

    * **Google Sheets:** We maintain financial and issuance logs via Google Sheets. We log the date, your full name,
      card number, host institution, nationality, payment method, and amount paid.

    * **Google Wallet:** If you add your pass to Google Wallet, we transmit your name, photo, date of birth,
      nationality, and mobility details to Google to generate the secure digital object.

    * **Apple Wallet:** If you add your pass to an iOS device, similar pass data (name, nationality, DOB, photo) is
      formatted for Apple Wallet. We use the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to send background updates to your
      passes if your membership details change.

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights {#your-data-protection-rights}

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

* **Access:** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

* **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data.

* **Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** Request immediate deletion of your data before our automated 1-year retention
  period, provided there is no legal obligation to retain it.

* **Restriction & Objection:** Object to or restrict the processing of your data.

## 6. Contact Us {#contact-us}

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, how your data is handled, or if you wish to exercise your data
rights, please contact the Web Projects Administrator of ESN Cyprus.
