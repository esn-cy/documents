# 6. General Notes {#general-notes}

* 6.1 Wherever there is a reference to the National Arbitration Board, until it is officially formed, its competences
  will be exercised by the National Board. In case a member of the National Board is involved in a report, then the
  competent body in still the National Board excluding the involved member,

* 6.2 This document should be interpreted and applied in conjunction to the latest form of the Statutes.

* 6.3 In case of removal of a member from ESN Cyprus/Nicosia and/or removal from a position (as laid down in Article 4.5
  of the present document), then the role of the National Arbitration Board is advisory to the National Board, in
  accordance to the Statutes' provisions.
