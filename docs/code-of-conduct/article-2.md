# 2. Scope of Application {#scope-of-application}

* 2.1. The behaviours outlined in the Code of Conduct apply to all volunteers of ESN, students and third people when
  they engage in any activity related to ESN.

* 2.2 'Activity' should be interpreted broadly and covers, but is not limited to, the following:
    * Meetings, gatherings or events, physical or online;
    * Online communication, through email or instant messaging;
    * External representation including non ESN related events.
