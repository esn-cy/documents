# 4. Procedures {#procedures}

* 4.1 Reporting a (suspected) violation

    Enforcing the Code of Conduct can only be done if violations are reported, therefore everyone is encouraged to report
    a suspected violation if they witness it or become aware of it. The procedures for reporting a (suspected) violation
    are elaborated on below.

* 4.2 Duration of an event

    During events of ESN Cyprus/Nicosia, the (suspected) violations are reported in first instance to the Point of
    Contact, which consists of the following people:

    * a. Member of the Organising Committee of the event,
    * b. Member of the National Board,
    * c. Member of the National Arbitration Board (when it is created).

    A report to the Point of Contact should always be followed by a report through the report form as described further in
    the next paragraph.

* 4.3 After the event and/or other situations

    The initial report should be executed towards the Point of Contact, however, it is not mandatory, shall the volunteer
    not feel confident. In both cases, violations should be reported through the report form, which can be found on
    this [link](https://forms.gle/qZQfjJBZoEa3BRY5A).

* 4.4 Anti-Retaliation Policy

    When reporting an incident it is important for our members to feel safe. Retaliatory behaviour against anyone who, in
    good faith, reports a concern or participates in an incident report can be subject to similar sanctions, as described
    in the following article, regardless if allegations are ultimately substantiated.

* 4.5 Procedure after the Report

    The following guidelines are only followed to the extent that is appropriate for each situation, because each case is
    different and the steps described are not always suitable.

    * a. Confidentiality between the reporter and the Arbitration Board is established. Any information or action-taking
    from this point onwards needs to be agreed upon by the reporter of the case.
    * b. Relevant parties are identified and inquired for further information. Other potentially relevant evidence is
    also collected.
    * c. The reportee gets the opportunity to plead their case to the Arbitration Board in the form of a conference. The
    initial report as well as relevant information that might be used for issuing a decision need to be addressed.
    * d. The Arbitration Board decides the next steps e.g. sanctioning, mediation. The reporter and reportee are
    informed in accordance to the provisions of the Charter.
