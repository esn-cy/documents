# Chapter B - Membership Status {#chapter-b-membership-status}

## 6. Members - Registration {#members-registration}

* 6.1. The members of the Association are divided into the following categories:

    * 6.1.1. Candidate Member
    * 6.1.2. Active Member
    * 6.1.3. Alumnus Member

* 6.2. Any student, student participating in an Erasmus+ programme, graduate of a University in Cyprus, or any person
  who studied abroad and accepts the principles and purposes of ESN International, as set out in this Statute and in the
  Statute of ESN International, may become a Candidate Member of the Association.

* 6.3. The procedure for validating a Candidate Member as an ActiveMember is as follows:

    * 6.3.1. Twice a year, before the beginning of the academic semesters, an announcement regarding the possibility of
      registering with the Association is published on Social Media. Persons who have expressed interest are invited to
      participate in introductory training seminars, which are organised by the Vice President for Administration -
      Secretary.
    * 6.3.2. Candidate Members must attend 50% plus one of the introductory training seminars. The number of training
      seminars ranges from three (3) to five (5) - the number of seminars is determined by the Vice President for
      Administration - Secretary, taking into account the number of interested persons and the training material
      offered.
    * 6.3.3. Following completion of the introductory training seminars, a trial period follows, lasting from two (2)
      weeks to one (1) month. During the trial period, Candidate Members contribute to events by participating in
      Organising Committees.
    * 6.3.4. At the end of the trial period, the Executive Board votes on the validation of each Candidate Member as a
      Regular Member.

* 6.4. The criteria taken into account by the Executive Board when validating a Candidate Member as an Active Member
  include, among others, the following:

    * 6.4.1. Demonstration of willingness to offer assistance before and during events,
    * 6.4.2. Participation and positive performance in an Organising Committee,
    * 6.4.3. Decision-making and taking initiatives,
    * 6.4.4. Team spirit and knowledge of various languages,
    * 6.4.5. Organisational skills,
    * 6.4.6. Previous experience in similar organisations.

* 6.5. Each regular member must register on the Galaxy platform provided by ESN International.

* 6.6. New Active Members must be validated by the next Ordinary General Assembly.

* 6.7. During the evaluation of candidate members, no form of discrimination should be created, for example in relation
  to sex, sexual orientation, disability, age, race, national origin, ethnic origin or religion.

* 6.8. Where a candidate member wishes to become an Active Member and their application has been rejected by the
  Executive Board, the candidate member may appeal to the General Assembly, which is obliged to examine the matter and
  make a final decision.

* 6.9. Active Members are the members with full rights and obligations and are also those who shall be counted towards
  the minimum number of 20 members required for an Association to exist.

* 6.0. Alumni Members are declared to be members who made a valuable and honourable contribution to the Association in
  the past but are no longer counted as regular members. Their declaration may be made either on the initiative of the
  Executive Board or upon application by the members themselves to the General Assembly. In both cases, the decision to
  declare honorary members is the responsibility of the General Assembly.

* 6.11. Alumni Members have the right to submit a candidacy to take part in the National Advisory Body.

* 6.12. The registration fee for Active Members is set at €0. The annual subscription is set at €0.

## 7. Obligations of Active Members {#obligations-of-active-members}

* 7.1. The members of the Association are obliged:

    * 7.1.1. To make every effort to achieve its purposes, to comply with the provisions of the present Statute and to
      protect the interests of the Association in every way.
    * 7.1.2. To attend the General Assemblies, ordinary and extraordinary, when called by the Administration, and to
      comply with the decisions of the General Assemblies and the Executive Board.

## 8. Rights of Regular Members {#rights-of-regular-members}

* 8.1. All regular members of the Association have equal rights and membership is not capable of representation - it is
  neither transferable nor inheritable.

* 8.2. Each member has the following rights:

    * 8.2.1. Regular members of the Association have the right to attend the General Assemblies, take the floor, express
      and discuss their views on the items on the agenda. They also have the right to participate in the events of the
      Association.
    * 8.2.2. The right to vote and to stand for election to the Administrative Bodies, as well as to vote for decisions
      of the General Assemblies to be taken.

## 9. Admission - Withdrawal - Expulsion of Members {#admission-withdrawal-expulsion-of-members}

* 9.1. The admission of new members is always permitted provided that the new members meet the criteria and the
  procedures described in point 6.3 are followed.

* 9.2. Members are entitled to withdraw from the Association at any time.

* 9.3. An Active Member of the Association may be expelled only following a proposal for removal at one of the ordinary
  General Assemblies, by an absolute majority of 2/3 of the present members, following the procedures set out in this
  Article.

* 9.4. Expulsion of a member is permitted where the member, through their overall conduct, acts or omissions, brings
  about or causes humiliation or a reduction in the credibility or prestige of the Association or other damage to its
  interests.

* 9.5. In addition to the reasons referred to in point 9.4 of this Article, a member may also be expelled for any of the
  following reasons:

    * 9.5.1. Acts and/or omissions that conflict with the principles and purposes of ESN.
    * 9.5.2. Absence from 3 consecutive ordinary General Assemblies. The Executive Board is obliged to inform the member
      concerned of the possible removal at the 4th ordinary General Assembly from which they are absent.
    * 9.5.3. Violation of the Association's Code of Conduct.
    * 9.5.4. Absence from 3 consecutive Section Meetings. The Executive Board has to inform the Active Member of their
      potential expulsion ahead of the next Ordinary General Assembly.
    * 9.5.5. For reasons which the Executive Board considers important and which it must present to the General
      Assembly.

* 9.6. If the required majority does not exist, the member is granted a one-month extension to comply and the member is
  placed under supervision. At the end of the month, the vote is repeated, this time requiring a simple majority.

## 10. Rights and Obligations of Members who Withdraw {#rights-and-obligations-of-members-who-withdraw}

* 10.1. A member of the Association who withdraws has no right over the property of the Association.
