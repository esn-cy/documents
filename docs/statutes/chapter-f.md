# Chapter F - National Advisory Body {#chapter-f-national-advisory-body}

## 21. National Advisory Body {#national-advisory-body}

* 21.1. The National Advisory Body consists of three (3) persons who are alumni members of the Association.

* 21.2. The term of office of this body is one (1) year, and it is elected by a General Assembly of the Members and is
  constituted as follows: Chief Advisor, Legal Advisor, Governance Advisor.

## 22. Method of Election and Removal of the National Advisory Body {#method-of-election-and-removal-of-the-national-advisory-body}

* 22.1. The members of the National Advisory Body are elected by the General Assembly on the basis of the following
  procedure:

    * 22.1.1. All honorary members of the Association who, during their active service in the Association, held a role
      within the Executive Board have the right to apply for a position on the National Advisory Body.
    * 22.1.2. Interested candidates must declare their interest in the position to the Executive Board by completing an
      expression-of-interest application.
    * 22.1.3. The Executive Board must approve the expressions of interest and propose the approved candidates to the
      General Assembly.
    * 22.1.4. Candidates for the positions must be announced to the members at least one week before the scheduled
      elections. Where a member of the Body needs to be elected at an Extraordinary General Assembly, the candidates
      must be announced and publicised at least 4 days before the elections.
    * 22.1.5. For the election of each member of the Executive Board, a simple majority of the members present is
      required, taking into account also those participating by teleconference. Abstentions are not counted in favour of
      the candidates.
    * 22.1.6. If there is no candidacy for a position, the position remains open and elections for that specific
      position are scheduled again for the next ordinary meeting.
    * 22.1.7. If two or more candidates receive the same number of votes, the vote is repeated.

* 22.2. The election of the members of the National Advisory Body takes place during an Ordinary General Assembly every
  July.

* 22.3. A transition period of 3 weeks is provided by the outgoing members of the National Advisory Body. During this
  period, information and guidance are provided on the use of communication and action tools, and pending matters are
  assigned to the newly elected National Advisory Body.

* 22.4. Removal of the members of the National Advisory Body takes place on the basis of the following procedure:

    * 22.4.1. A proposal to remove a member of the National Advisory Body may be submitted by ⅓ of the active members or
      by at least 2 other members of the Executive Board.
    * 22.4.2. The member is called upon to provide explanations at the next Ordinary General Assembly. If at least ⅔ of
      the Ordinary General Assembly, by absolute majority, vote in favour of removal, the member is removed from their
      position without also being expelled from the Association.

* 22.5. Removal of a member of the Executive Board arises for the following reasons:

    * 22.5.1. Conviction by a Court for any offence.
    * 22.5.2. Obtaining financial benefit from the activities or property of the Association.
    * 22.5.3. Disobedience to the decisions of the General Assembly.
    * 22.5.4. Violation of the Association's Code of Conduct.

## 23. Method of Operation of the National Advisory Body {#method-of-operation-of-the-national-advisory-body}

* 23.1. The scope of action and decision-making of the National Advisory Body is limited as set out below:

    * 23.1.1. Resolution of complaints and/or violations arising from the Association's Code of Conduct,
    * 23.1.2. Provision of advice and guidelines following submission of a query by the Executive Board.

* 23.2. The role of the Body is advisory and arbitral in nature, and no body is bound by its decisions.

* 23.3. The members of the National Advisory Body each have the following duties and powers:

    * 23.3.1. The Chief Advisor undertakes the conduct of procedures in accordance with the Statute, the Code of Conduct
      and the Law, and also coordinates the process between the parties and the Body.
    * 23.3.2. The Legal Advisor is responsible for the compatibility of decisions with the Law and the Statute, as well
      as for determining the admissibility of evidence
    * 23.3.3. The Research Advisor is responsible for conducting research into the evidence and interviewing the persons
      concerned, as well as for preparing the final reports and minutes.

* 23.4. The National Advisory Body operates continuously on the principles of impartiality, confidentiality and
  practical effectiveness.

## 24. Convening of the National Advisory Body {#convening-of-the-national-advisory-body}

* 24.1. The National Advisory Body must be convened at least two (2) times per semester and whenever a query or
  complaint arises, on the basis of the following procedure:

    * 24.1.1. The members of the Body must, in consultation with one another, decide the time and place for convening
      the Council - it may also be held online.
    * 24.1.2. For this decision, the Chief Advisor is obliged to officially inform the remaining members at least 2 days
      in advance.

* 24.2. The National Advisory Body has a quorum with the presence of two of the three members.

* 24.3. The hearing and decision-making procedure must be followed as set out in the Code of Conduct.

* 24.4. For queries from the Executive Board, the National Advisory Body is obliged to respond within seven (7) days.
