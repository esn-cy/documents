# Chapter D - General Assembly {#chapter-d-general-assembly}

## 12. General Assembly {#general-assembly}

* 12.1. The assembly of the members of the Association constitutes the supreme body of the Association and decides on
  every matter of the Association which does not fall within the competence of another body. The competences and
  procedures for convening it are defined in Article 13.

* 12.2. The General Assembly may be ordinary or extraordinary.

* 12.3. At least two (2) General Assemblies must be convened each year.

## 13. Decisions and Manner of Convening General Assemblies {#decisions-and-manner-of-convening-general-assemblies}

* 13.1. The assembly has the following competences:

    * 13.1.1. The election of the members of the National Council and other elected positions.
    * 13.1.2. The appointment of the auditors of the Association's accounts.
    * 13.1.3. The acceptance, rejection or expulsion of a member, or the downgrading of regular members, as well as the
      upgrading of members under supervision to regular members.
    * 13.1.4. Taking decisions to ensure the smooth operation of the network.
    * 13.1.5. The approval of the balance sheet.
    * 13.1.6. The alteration of the purpose of the Association.
    * 13.1.7. The amendment of the Statute.
    * 13.1.8. The dissolution of the Association.
    * 13.1.9. The endorsement of candidate regular members for positions on the International Board.
    * 13.1.10. Taking decisions regarding the National Objectives (Action Plan).
    * 13.1.11. The organisation of at least one event aimed at incoming students.

* 13.2. The assembly of members convenes once every academic four-month period, at a place and time determined by the
  Executive Board of the Association in the following cases and/or when the interests of the Association so require:

    * 13.2.1. For the conduct of elections.
    * 13.2.2. For the appointment of the auditors of the Association's accounts.
    * 13.2.3. For the acceptance, rejection, expulsion, downgrading or upgrading of members.
    * 13.2.4. To ensure the smooth operation of the network.
    * 13.2.5. For the approval of the balance sheet.
    * 13.2.6. For the alteration of the purpose of the Association, the amendment of the Statute or the dissolution of
      the Association.
    * 13.2.7. For the endorsement of candidate regular members for positions at international level.
    * 13.2.8. For taking decisions regarding the National Objectives.
    * 13.2.9. For information and discussion on matters of ESN International.
    * 13.2.10. For the preparation of members for international conferences or seminars.
    * 13.2.11. For the delivery of Training seminars to strengthen the capacities of the Association.

* 13.3. The assembly of the members of the Association has the competence to supervise and control the members of the
  Executive Board and is entitled to remove such members in the following cases:

    * 13.3.1. Unjustified absence from 4 Ordinary and Extraordinary General Assemblies.
    * 13.3.2. Conviction by a Court for any offence.
    * 13.3.3. Obtaining financial benefit from the activities or property of the Association.
    * 13.3.4. Disobedience to the decisions of the General Assembly.

* 13.4. An Extraordinary General Assembly of the members is convened in the same cases in which the ordinary General
  Assembly is convened and/or when the interests of the Association so require, and follows the same rules and
  procedures as the General Assembly.

* 13.5. An Extraordinary General Assembly is convened by the Executive Board according to the following procedure:

    * 13.5.1. The Executive Board must notify the convening of the Extraordinary General Assembly 7 days in
      advance through the official communication channels of the Association, as well as the place and time
    * 13.5.2. The Executive Board must also notify the matters to be discussed
    * 13.5.6. An Extraordinary General Assembly may be convened by one fifth of the members, by written request stating
      the matters to be discussed as well as the place and time. A representative of the members must inform both the
      Executive Board and the remaining members through the official communication channels of the Association.
    * 13.5.7. A member is not entitled to participate either in the discussion or in the vote if the decision to be
      taken concerns the conclusion of a legal transaction or the bringing or discontinuance of legal proceedings
      between the Association and that member, or the member's spouse or a relative by blood or affinity up to and
      including the third degree, or the conclusion of a legal transaction between the Association and a company,
      whether a partnership or a capital company, in which or in whose management that member, the member's spouse or a
      relative by blood or affinity up to and including the third degree participates.
    * 13.5.8. A General Assembly shall be deemed to have a quorum if 50% plus one of the regular members are present,
      taking into account also those participating by teleconference. This number may not be less than 11.
    * 13.5.9. Decisions of the assembly of the members of the Association are taken by a majority of the members
      present, taking into account also those participating by teleconference. A decision of the assembly on a matter
      which was not stated in the invitation is invalid.
    * 13.5.10. Each Active Member has one vote.
    * 13.5.11. For all other matters, a simple majority of the members present is sufficient.
    * 13.5.12. Voting regarding the formation of the Executive Board and other elected positions is always secret. For
      any other matter, voting is open.
    * 13.5.13. In the event of a secret ballot, the votes are collected by a three-member electoral committee (Chairing
      Team), composed of non-candidate members of the Association and elected on the spot. The three-member electoral
      committee has the right to vote.
    * 13.5.14. Members have the right to cast a positive or negative vote, or to abstain in elections. Abstentions are
      considered neither positive nor negative.
    * 13.5.15. The attendance of members at the General Assembly is recorded by completing their details and signing a
      form.

## 14. Amendment of the Statute - Dissolution - Alteration of the Purpose of the Association {#amendment-of-the-statute-dissolution-alteration-of-the-purpose-of-the-association}

* 14.1. For a decision to amend the Statute or dissolve the Association or alter its purpose, the consent of three
  quarters of all members of the Association is required (persons participating by teleconference are also counted).
