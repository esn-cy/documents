# Notes {#notes}

The present Statute, consisting of 30 Articles, was read at the General Assembly of the Association on 02/11/2025 and
was approved.

The provisions of the amendments to the present Statutes enter into force on the 1st of January 2026, and apply to any
matter that arises from this date onwards.

The fidelity of the present copy is confirmed.
