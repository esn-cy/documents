# Chapter A - General {#chapter-a-general}

## 1. Establishment - District - Registration {#establishment-district-registration}

* 1.1. A non-profit Association is hereby established under the name Erasmus Student Network Cyprus (ESN Cyprus), with
  its registered office in the Municipality of Nicosia, Nicosia District, at 28 Makariou III Avenue, 1065, Nicosia,
  Cyprus.

* 1.2. Based on the present Statute, the principal body of the members of the Erasmus Student Network (also known as
  Erasmus Student Network AISBL) officially established in Cyprus is Erasmus Student Network Cyprus (ESN Cyprus).

## 2. Seal - Emblem {#seal-emblem}

* 2.1. The Association uses the organisation's emblem as its seal.

* 2.2. The emblem of the Association consists of the ESN star, which is placed in the upper left part, and the acronym
  “ESN”, which is located to the right of the star, in black, in a typeface created and provided by ESN International.
  Beneath them, the full name of the organisation “Erasmus Student Network” is written in black, in Word Sans typeface.
  At the bottom, the word “Cyprus” is written, also in Word Sans typeface and in black. The logo, colours and typeface
  comply with the rules for “Visual Identity” in the “Visual Identity Manual” published by ESN International.

## 3. Purposes {#purposes}

* 3.1. ESN Cyprus operates, on the one hand, in the interest of students who undertake all and/or part of their
  education or further education at higher education institutions outside their country of origin and, on the other
  hand, in the interest of students of universities which receive such students. ESN Cyprus promotes
  internationalisation in the field of education. The main purposes of ESN Cyprus are the following:

    * 3.1.1. The harmonious, social and practical integration of newly-arrived international students into the Cypriot
      host Universities, Cypriot everyday life, culture and traditions,
    * 3.1.2. Representation of the needs and expectations of international students at local, national and international
      level,
    * 3.1.3. Provision of information regarding academic exchange programmes and the ways in which ESN can contribute,
    * 3.1.4. Reintegration of students returning to their home country through the creation and promotion of
      opportunities for involvement in the network,
    * 3.1.5. Contribution to the evaluation of the various exchange programmes with the aim of improving academic
      mobility,
    * 3.1.6. Encouraging students to spend part of their higher education in another state,
    * 3.1.7. Provision of social and practical assistance to students during their mobility period,
    * 3.1.8. Provision of opportunities for intercultural understanding, training and self-development.
    * 3.1.9. The provision of information, as well as encouragement and mobilisation with the aim of increasing the rate
      of student participation in European Programmes.

* 3.2. The actions of the organisation must not in any way conflict with the principles set out in the present Statute,
  provided that the provisions of the Associations and Foundations and Other Related Matters Law of 2017 shall prevail.

## 4. Operating Principles {#operating-principles}

* 4.1. ESN Cyprus is a voluntary organisation, without any political, financial and/or religious interest, and operates
  without:

    * 4.1.1. Affiliation with political parties or systems,
    * 4.1.2. Commitment to specific political views or ideologies,
    * 4.1.3. Affiliation with religious or denominational groups.

* 4.2. ESN Cyprus promotes cooperation and coexistence among people in a united Europe and promotes Cypriot Culture.

* 4.3. ESN Cyprus maintains the positive image of the organisation by refraining from harmful depictions of nudity,
  intoxication, drug use, any form of hate speech, physical assault or any other behaviour that is inconsistent with the
  values and purposes of the Erasmus Student Network.

## 5. Means for Achieving the Purposes {#means-for-achieving-the-purposes}

* 5.1. The means for achieving the purposes of the Association are the organisation of activities aimed at incoming
  Erasmus students or students of other European and International programmes, regular students of Cypriot universities
  and persons interested in participating in exchange programmes.

* 5.2. Such activities may include, among others:

    * 5.2.1. Conferences and presentations for the promotion of mobility,
    * 5.2.2. Meetings and close cooperation with Cypriot Universities and the Erasmus offices of those Universities,
    * 5.2.3. Participation in conferences and events organised by other bodies,
    * 5.2.4. Excursions for the promotion of Cypriot culture and civilisation to students,
    * 5.2.5. Film screenings and other social gatherings for the smooth integration of incoming students,
    * 5.2.6. Participation in conferences and training seminars of ESN International.
