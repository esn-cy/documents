# Chapter G - Financial Resources and Property {#chapter-g-financial-resources-and-property}

## 25. Financial Control of the Association {#financial-control-of-the-association}

* 25.1. The audit of the accounts and supervision of the Association with regard to the financial management of the
  Association's income is carried out by the Auditor. The Auditor is elected by the General Assembly on the same day on
  which the election of the Executive Board is held, and their term of office is the same as that of the Executive
  Board. The Auditor may not hold any other position either on the Executive Board or as a Project Coordinator, nor be
  related to any other elected member.

* 25.2. The Auditor has the duty to inspect the financial activities during the year, without, however, judging the
  expediency of the Board's discretion, but only the outer limits thereof. The members are obliged to provide the
  Auditor with all necessary information. The results of the audit are presented no later than at the final General
  Assembly and must be approved by resolution.

* 25.3. A bank account is maintained in the name of the Association within the Republic of Cyprus, into which the monies
  of the Association's treasury must be deposited, except for an amount for the immediate operational needs of the
  Association. The persons responsible for the account are the President, the Treasurer and the Vice President of the
  Board.

* 25.4. For financial activities whose value in total exceeds 10% of the annual budget, the following restrictions
  apply:

    * 25.4.1. For those activities, the Executive Board must obtain at least 2 quotations from at least 2 businesses.
    * 25.4.2. For those activities, the President and the Treasurer must sign a contract describing the agreement. They
      are only authorised to sign a contract describing what has been agreed at the meeting of the Association.
    * 25.4.3. Where contracts contain additional information, the additional information shall be considered invalid.
    * 25.4.4. During votes concerning financial activity, all parties believed to have interests that conflict with the
      financial activity must abstain from the vote.

* 25.5. The Association manages its resources according to its own initiatives, subject only to the principles of ESN
  International and the laws of the State.

* 25.6. All international agreements of ESN Cyprus are automatically also considered local agreements. A contract must
  be created bearing the 2 signatures of the National Representative (NR) or President or Treasurer and the other party.

* 25.7. For any financial activity carried out, the Association must benefit in accordance with the spirit of the
  principles of ESN International.

* 25.8. There must be accountability for every financial activity and transparency must be maintained.

## 26. Financial Resources {#financial-resources}

* 26.1. The property of the Association is:

    * 26.1.1. Money held in a bank account
    * 26.1.2. Cash for facilitating transactions
    * 26.1.3. Furniture and office equipment
    * 26.1.4. Consumables (used during the organisation of events)
    * 26.1.5. Stock of promotional products and gifts

* 26.2. The sources of income of the Association are:

    * 26.2.1. Sale of ESNcards
    * 26.2.2. Sponsorships from higher education institutions and other governmental and non-governmental bodies for the
      carrying out of activities related to the purposes of the Association. Income from the activities organised by the
      Association.
    * 26.2.3. Commissions from the promotion of the Association's partners.
    * 26.2.4. Income from the sale of promotional products.

* 26.3. The Association provides ESNcards to all registered students of its universities who participate or have
  participated in a student exchange programme.

* 26.4. If the Association receives a registration form from an Erasmus student whose university does not cooperate with
  the Association, the Association may provide that student with an ESNcard.

* 26.5. A member of the Association may provide ESNcards to Cypriots who meet the conditions of the present Statute. The
  only restriction is that the member must fully inform the student about any existing branches - members and about
  their rights.

* 26.6. The price for distribution of the ESNcard is determined in each city.

## 27. Budget {#budget}

* 27.1. The financial year of the Association begins on 1 September.

* 27.2. The budget of the Association is prepared by the Executive Board and submitted for approval by vote at the
  annual General Assembly. The budget of the Association includes the income and expenses of the Association as well as
  an explanation for each of them.

## 28. Dissolution of the Association {#dissolution-of-the-association}

* 28.1. The Association is dissolved if the number of its active members is below 20\.

* 28.2. At any time following a decision of the General Assembly, which decision shall be taken by a majority of the
  registered members of the Association. The dissolution of the Association requires the consent of three quarters of
  all members of the Association. In cases where the presence of three quarters of the members becomes practically
  difficult, the Registrar may grant consent for the submission of an application to the Court for the issue of an order
  enabling the applicant Association to proceed with the decision-making process in the presence of at least up to two
  fifths of the total number of its members.

* 28.3. In the event of dissolution, after any debts of the Association have first been paid, the remaining property of
  the Association shall pass to an Association with similar purposes and under no circumstances may it be distributed
  among its members.
