# Chapter E - Executive Board {#chapter-e-executive-board}

## 15. Executive Board {#executive-board}

* 15.1. The Erasmus Student Network (ESN Cyprus) is administered by an Executive Board consisting of seven (7) persons
  who are regular members of the Association and are elected by the General Assembly of its members.

* 15.2. The Executive Board shall be constituted as follows: President, Vice President for Coordination, Vice President
  for Administration - Secretary, Treasurer, Communications Officer, Web Projects Administrator and National
  Representative.

* 15.3. The mandate of the Executive Board lasts for one (1) year.

## 16. Method of Election and Removal of Members of the Executive Board {#method-of-election-and-removal-of-members-of-the-executive-board}

* 16.1. The members of the Executive Board are elected by the General Assembly on the basis of the following procedure:

    * 16.1.1. All active members of the Association who have been members for at least six months have the right to
      apply for a position on the Executive Board. Experience in other Associations of ESN International is also
      counted.
    * 16.1.2. Candidates for positions on the Executive Board must be announced to the members at least one week before
      the scheduled elections. Where a member of the Board needs to be elected at a General Assembly, the candidates
      must be announced and publicised at least 4 days before the elections.
    * 16.1.3. For the election of each member of the Executive Board, a simple majority of the members present is
      required, taking into account also those participating by teleconference. In order to be entitled to vote, members
      must have attended at least half of the candidates' presentations. Abstentions are not counted in favour of the
      candidates.
    * 16.1.4. If there is no candidacy for a position, the position remains open and elections for that specific
      position are scheduled again for the next ordinary meeting.
    * 16.1.5. If two or more candidates receive the same number of votes, the vote is repeated. The options on the
      ballot should be yes and no.

* 16.2. The election of the members of the Executive Board takes place during an Ordinary General Assembly every June.

* 16.3. A transition period of at least 3 weeks is provided by the outgoing members of the Executive Board. During this
  period, information and guidance are provided on the use of communication and action tools, and pending matters are
  assigned to the newly elected Executive Board.

* 16.4. Removal of the members of the Executive Board takes place on the basis of the following procedure:

    * 16.4.1. A proposal to remove a member of the Executive Board may be submitted by ⅓ of the active members or by at
      least 2 other members of the Executive Board.
    * 16.4.2. The member is called upon to provide explanations at the next General Assembly. If at least ⅔ of the
      Ordinary General Assembly, by absolute majority, vote in favour of removal, the member is removed from their
      position without also being expelled from the Association.

* 16.5. Removal of a member of the Executive Board arises for the following reasons:

    * 16.5.1. Unjustified absence from 4 Ordinary and Extraordinary General Assemblies.
    * 16.5.2. Conviction by a Court for any offence.
    * 16.5.3. Obtaining financial benefit from the activities or property of the Association.
    * 16.5.4. Disobedience to the decisions of the General Assembly.
    * 16.5.5. Violation of the Association's Code of Conduct.

## 17. Decisions and Convening of the Executive Board {#decisions-and-convening-of-the-executive-board}

* 17.1. The Executive Board must be convened 2 times per semester on the basis of the following procedure:

    * 17.1.1. The President of the Board or two other members of the Board must, in consultation with the remaining
      members, decide the time and place for convening the Board.
    * 17.1.2. For this decision, the President is obliged to inform the remaining members at least 7 days in advance.

* 17.2. The Executive Board has a quorum when 50% plus one (1) of the members of the Board are present, including
  members participating by teleconference.

* 17.3. The Executive Board takes decisions on the following matters:

    * 17.3.1. Decisions to ensure the general smooth operation of the network,
    * 17.3.2. Decisions on representation matters at local, national and international level,
    * 17.3.3. Decisions on the management of resources, which shall be disclosed through the budget to the General
      Assembly,
    * 17.3.4. Proposal for the acceptance, downgrading or expulsion of members. The proposal must be accepted by the
      General Assembly.

* 17.4. A member of the Executive Board is not entitled to participate in the discussion or vote if the decision to be
  taken concerns the conclusion of a legal transaction or the bringing or discontinuance of legal proceedings between
  the Association and that member or the member's spouse or a relative by blood or affinity up to and including the
  third degree, or concerns the conclusion of a legal transaction between the Association and a company, whether a
  partnership or a capital company, in which or in whose management that member, the member's spouse or a relative by
  blood or affinity up to and including the third degree participates.

  It is understood that, before the beginning of the meeting, the member of the Executive Board declares the conflict of
  interest and withdraws.

* 17.5. Minutes are kept for the decisions of the Executive Board.

* 17.6. Decisions of the Executive Board are taken by an absolute majority of the members present, unless otherwise
  provided in the Statute, by open vote. In the event of a tie, the President's vote prevails.

## 18. Duties and Powers of Members of the Executive Board {#duties-and-powers-of-members-of-the-executive-board}

* 18.1. The Executive Board is obliged to handle diligently the affairs of the Association/Foundation/Federation or
  Union, in compliance with the laws of the Republic.

* 18.2. No remuneration of any kind is paid for services provided to any member or officer of this administration.

* 18.3. It is understood that the members, including members of the administration or founders, may recover and/or claim
  any reasonable expenses they incurred in the performance of their duties, including remuneration corresponding to
  research or other programmes funded or co-funded by third parties, upon presentation of the necessary documents,
  provided that the statute of the association does not provide otherwise.

* 18.4. The members of the Executive Board have the following duties and powers:

    * 18.4.1. President

        * 18.4.1.1. As the chief executive officer of the Association, convenes, directs and/or chairs all meetings of
          the Executive Board and generally represents the Association.
        * 18.4.1.2. Signs the minutes of the Executive Board, every document concerning the Association's relations with
          third parties and generally every document substantially concerning the operation of the Association.
        * 18.4.1.3. Ensures the implementation of decisions taken at meetings of the Executive Board and at other
          assemblies of the Association.
        * 18.4.1.4. Prepares and announces the matters for discussion for the General and Extraordinary General
          Assemblies.
        * 18.4.1.5. Acts as the external representative of the Association before universities and other national and/or
          local bodies.
        * 18.4.1.6. Represents the Association in judicial and extrajudicial matters.
        * 18.4.1.7. Has general supervision and coordination of the Association.
        * 18.4.1.8. Is obliged, in cooperation with the National Representative, to ensure the representation of the
          country at the General Assemblies of ESN International, by attending the meeting personally, by sending
          another member of the Association, or at least by granting voting authorisation to another attendee, in
          accordance with the predetermined procedures of ESN International.
        * 18.4.1.9. Deals with the field of Education and directs campaigns and initiatives launched by ESN
          International.

    * 18.4.2. Vice President for Administration - Secretary

        * 18.4.2.1. Together with the President, undertakes the expansion of the Association's networking through
          communication with other social actors.
        * 18.4.2.2. Initiates the procedures for the “recruitment” of new members, including the campaign, introductory
          training seminars and the onboarding process of new members.
        * 18.4.2.3. Organises training seminars and workshops for all members personally and/or in cooperation with
          other members of the Association.
        * 18.4.2.4. Ensures the maintenance of the mental and physical well-being of the members, as well as harmonious
          relations among the members.
        * 18.4.2.5. Co-signs with the President all outgoing documents and letters of the Association and keeps a record
          of all incoming and outgoing correspondence of the Association.
        * 18.4.2.6. Maintains a Register of members, which is continuously updated.
        * 18.4.2.7. Is responsible for safeguarding the books of Minutes of the meetings of the Executive Board, the
          Registration Certificate and generally the records of the Association.
        * 18.4.2.8. Is responsible for safeguarding the seal of the Association, with which all outgoing documents and
          letters are sealed.
        * 18.4.2.9. Deals with the field of Human Resources and Network Care in ESN International, while promoting the
          integration of initiatives launched within the Network.

    * 18.4.3. Vice President for Coordination

        * 18.4.3.1. Preparation of the programme of local events for the semester and/or the entire year.
        * 18.4.3.2. Organisation, supervision and conduct of the Association's events and excursions through the
          formation of organising committees.
        * 18.4.3.3. Supervision and coordination of national and international events.
        * 18.4.3.4. Ensures that the events conducted follow at least one of the values of ESN.
        * 18.4.3.5. Undertakes the registration of the Association's events on the website proposed by ESN
          International.
        * 18.4.3.6. Deals with the field of Activities and Education of ESN International, while promoting the
          integration of initiatives launched within the Network.

    * 18.4.4. Treasurer

        * 18.4.4.1. Is responsible for the management of the property of the Association.
        * 18.4.4.2. Ensures the keeping of the accounting books and accounts required by law.
        * 18.4.4.3. Submits to the Executive Board at the end of each financial year the budget and balance sheet and is
          required to keep an account of all financial transactions of the Association, substantiating them with
          supporting documents (receipts, invoices, etc.) and recording them in the cash books.
        * 18.4.4.4. Makes all payments approved by the Executive Board for which the President has given authorisation
          and keeps all related receipts in a special file.
        * 18.4.4.5. Following a decision of the Executive Board, opens an account or accounts in the name of the
          Association with banks and/or other credit institutions.
        * 18.4.4.6. Co-signs, together with the President and/or the Vice President and/or the Secretary and/or any
          member of the Executive Board as the Executive Board may determine from time to time, the cheques issued and
          relating to the payment of the Association's obligations.
        * 18.4.4.7. Proposes the semi-annual and annual budget.
        * 18.4.4.8. Deals with the field of Business and Finance of ESN International and promotes initiatives launched
          within the Network.

    * 18.4.5. Communications Officer

        * 18.4.5.1. Planning and implementation of the media strategy.
        * 18.4.5.2. Management of the Association's Mass Media,
        * 18.4.5.3. Together with the Treasurer, design, production and promotion of promotional products,
        * 18.4.5.4. Care and attention to the image of ESN towards sponsors,
        * 18.4.5.5. Promotion of the Organisation to other organisations and stakeholders.
        * 18.4.5.6. Management of the “Communication Team”, in which the members are guided during the design and
          publication of events,
        * 18.4.5.7. Ensures and promotes the maintenance of the visual identity of the Erasmus Student Network, as
          defined in the relevant guidelines established by ESN International.
        * 18.4.5.8. Deals with the Communications field of ESN International and promotes initiatives launched within
          the Network.

    * 18.4.6. Web Projects Administrator

        * 18.4.6.1. Maintaining and updating the ESN Cyprus website, ensuring that its content is up-to-date and
          functional.
        * 18.4.6.2. Overseeing and managing ongoing digital projects, coordinating updates and improvements.
        * 18.4.6.3. Managing and maintaining the ESN Cyprus email domain, ensuring its smooth operation.
        * 18.4.6.4. Maintaining and updating the ESN Cyprus portal, fulfilling the organisation's operational
          requirements.
        * 18.4.6.5. Supporting members with digital platforms before, during, and after events.
        * 18.4.6.6. Troubleshooting technical issues and assisting members with the use of tools and platforms.
        * 18.4.6.7. Ensuring that the website, portal, and email systems comply with the organisation's standards and
          objectives.

    * 18.4.7. National Representative

        * 18.4.7.1. The National Representative represents Cyprus in ESN International and acts on the basis of the
          decisions of the Ordinary General Assembly and always acts for the benefit of the country and the Association.
        * 18.4.7.2. The National Representative must transfer information, decisions, proposals, amendments and any
          other matter concerning ESN Cyprus from ESN International to ESN Cyprus and vice versa, through the relevant
          communication media (the GA mailing list and the ESN Cyprus communication forum).
        * 18.4.7.3. Cooperates with the President of the Executive Board to ensure the representation of the country at
          the General Assemblies of ESN International, by attending the meeting personally, by sending another member of
          the Association, or at least by granting voting authorisation to another attendee, in accordance with the
          predetermined procedures of ESN International.

## 19. Duties and Powers of the Executive Board as a Whole {#duties-and-powers-of-the-executive-board-as-a-whole}

* 19.1. The Executive Board is obliged to handle diligently the affairs of the Association, in compliance with the laws
  of the Republic.

* 19.2. The Executive Board of the Association is obliged to maintain a fully updated register of its members, which is
  updated at least once a year and is available for inspection by the Registrar and by any third party having a
  legitimate interest

* 19.3. The Executive Board is obliged, no later than within one (1) month from ascertaining that the number of members
  has fallen below twenty (20), to notify this fact to the Registrar, also indicating the date on which this occurred.

* 19.4. Following the adoption of any amendment to the present Statute, the Executive Board is obliged to submit to the
  Registrar, without undue delay and in any event no later than thirty (30) days from the date the amendment was
  adopted, a written application for registration of the amendment in the Register

* 19.5. The Executive Board is obliged, within the first quarter of each year, to notify the Registrar of Associations
  in writing of the following:

    * 19.5.1. in numerical terms, any removals of members and registrations of new members that took place during the
      previous year.
    * 19.5.2. where changes have occurred, the current members of the administration of the Association, with their
      corresponding offices and contact details.
    * 19.5.3. whether, during the previous year, the minimum number of annual general assemblies prescribed by the
      Statute was held.
    * 19.5.4. in the event of a change in the address of the premises and/or the contact details of the Association, the
      new address and/or the new contact details as soon as the change occurs.

* 19.6. The Executive Board of the Association is obliged to keep accounting books in which all acts relating to all
  transactions of the Association are entered and, at the end of each financial year, to prepare the following accounts:

    * 19.6.1. An account of the gross income of the Association during the financial year;
    * 19.6.2. An account of its credit balance at the beginning of the financial year and of all money received on its
      behalf during that year;
    * 19.6.3. An account of all sums of money owed by it or owed to it and of the payments made during the same
      financial year

* 19.7. The accounts of the Association are audited by an approved auditor, unless the income of the Association does
  not exceed forty thousand euros (€40,000).

* 19.8. The members of the administration of the Association are obliged to submit to the Registrar, no later than seven
  (7) months after the end of the financial year, the accounts and the relevant report of the approved auditor

* 19.9. The Executive Board has the following duties and powers:

    * 19.9.1. The Executive Board is responsible for the smooth operation of the activities of the Association and has
      general supervision both of the actions of the members and of the national project coordinators.
    * 19.9.2. The Executive Board represents ESN Cyprus vis-à-vis ESN International.

* 19.10. Legal transactions carried out by the Executive Board of the Association within the limits of its authority
  bind the Association.

* 19.11. The members of the Executive Board are obliged to comply with and implement the provisions of the Prevention
  and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law of 2007, as amended.

## 20. Replacement of Members of the Executive Board {#replacement-of-members-of-the-executive-board}

* 20.1. A member of the Executive Board may resign, but is obliged to remain in office until the position is filled by a
  replacement, in any event for no longer than two weeks.

* 20.2. Every member of the Executive Board who resigns or is removed is obliged to provide information on the use of
  communication and action tools, as well as to transfer knowledge and assign pending matters from their term of office
  to their replacement, who is elected at the next ordinary assembly following the vacancy of the position.

* 20.3. Vacant positions on the Executive Board are filled as follows:

    * 20.3.1. The Executive Board is obliged to inform the members of the vacant position and to set a deadline for
      submission of applications by candidates, no later than two days after notification of the decision concerning the
      removal or resignation of the member.
    * 20.3.2. The Executive Board is obliged to convene the General Assembly so that a vote may take place, following
      the required procedure. The General Assembly must be held within two weeks from the date of notification of the
      decision concerning the removal or resignation of the member
    * 20.3.3. The term of office of the member lasts until the end of the normal term of office of the outgoing member.
    * 20.3.4. If the position remains vacant, the Executive Board assumes the duties of the position until it is filled.

* 20.4. If the vacant positions on the Executive Board exceed 50% plus one (1) of the members of the Board and/or the
  number of members falls below 5, elections are announced for the election of a new Executive Board.
