# Chapter H - Miscellaneous Provisions {#chapter-h-miscellaneous-provisions}

## 29. General Provisions {#general-provisions}

* 29.1. No remuneration of any kind is paid for services provided to any member or officer of its administration.

    * 29.1.1. It is understood that the members of the Executive Board of the Association may recover and/or claim any
      reasonable expenses they incurred in the performance of their duties, including remuneration corresponding to
      research or other programmes funded or co-funded by third parties, provided that they present the necessary
      documents.

* 29.2. The Association is represented judicially and extrajudicially by the President of the Executive Board.

* 29.3. The Association is liable towards third parties for any unlawful acts or omissions of the bodies or employees
  representing it which entail an obligation to pay compensation, provided that the harmful act or omission took place
  in the performance of the duties assigned to them. It is understood that, if the specific harmful act or omission was
  committed intentionally, fraudulently, in bad faith or due to gross negligence, the person or persons at fault are
  jointly and severally liable towards the Association for restoration of the damage suffered.

## 30. Interpretation of Articles {#interpretation-of-articles}

* 30.1. For every matter not provided for under the present Statute, as well as for every ambiguity, the General
  Assembly shall decide within the provisions of the legislation in force, namely the Associations and Foundations and
  Other Related Matters Laws of 2017 and 2018 (104 (I)/2017 and 76 (I)/2018), the Regulations issued thereunder and any
  future amendments thereto, the provisions of which shall prevail in every case where any ambiguity and/or
  contradiction arises.
