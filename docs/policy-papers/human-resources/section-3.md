# 3. Recruitment Process {#recruitment-process}

## 3.1 Application Form and Deadlines {#application-form-deadlines}

The application form for prospective members will remain continuously open throughout the academic year, allowing
interested individuals to submit an application at any time.

However, recruitment and onboarding will take place only twice per academic year. Therefore, applicants must ensure that
their application is submitted before the announced deadline for the recruitment period of the semester in which they
wish to participate.

The first recruitment period will normally begin with applications closing approximately during August, while the second
recruitment period will normally begin with applications closing approximately during December or January. The exact
deadlines for each recruitment period will be communicated in advance through the official communication channels of ESN
Nicosia.

All announced application deadlines must be strictly respected. Applications submitted after the relevant deadline will
not be considered for that recruitment period, even if the application form remains accessible. In such cases, the
application may only be considered for the following recruitment period.

It is the responsibility of each applicant to remain informed about the relevant deadline and submit their application
on time.

## 3.2 Interview Procedure {#interview-procedure}

Applicants whose applications are considered within the relevant recruitment period will proceed to an online interview
of approximately 30 minutes with the Vice President for Administration (VPA).

The interview serves as an introductory conversation between the applicant and ESN Nicosia. Applicants will have the
opportunity to introduce themselves, discuss their motivation and interests, and share what they hope to gain from or
contribute to their involvement in ESN.

At the same time, the interview provides applicants with the opportunity to learn more about ESN Nicosia, understand the
expectations and responsibilities associated with membership, and ask any questions they may have regarding the
organisation, volunteering experience or recruitment process.

The interview should therefore be approached as a two-way conversation, allowing both the VPA and the applicant to
determine whether the organisation and volunteering experience are suitable for the applicant before proceeding to the
next stage of recruitment.

## 3.3 Training Period {#training-period}

The onboarding process is designed to provide new volunteers with the knowledge, guidance and practical experience
necessary to understand ESN Cyprus and gradually become involved in the section.

As part of the recruitment process, three training sessions will be organised for new volunteers. Attendance at a
minimum of **50% + 1 of the training sessions are mandatory** in order to continue to the next stage of the recruitment
process.

Our trainings aim to introduce new volunteers to ESN, ESN Cyprus, the structure and values of the organisation, the
responsibilities associated with membership and the different opportunities available for involvement.

## 3.4 Trial Period {#trial-period}

Following the training period, new volunteers will enter a one-month trial period.

For members recruited during the first recruitment period, the trial period will normally take place during September.

For members recruited during the second recruitment period, the trial period will normally take place during January.

During this period, new volunteers are expected to attend ESN Nicosia events and become familiar with the section, its
members and the way activities are organised. The purpose of the trial period is to allow new volunteers to gradually
integrate into the community, experience the different types of activities organised by the section and understand the
responsibilities connected to becoming an active member.

During their trial period, new volunteers are not permitted to attend Ithaki. The purpose of this restriction is to
encourage new volunteers to prioritise ESN Nicosia's cultural, social and other organised activities during their first
month, allowing them to interact with Erasmus students and fellow volunteers in different environments and gain a
broader understanding of the section and its activities.

## 3.5 Organising Committee Participation {#organising-committee-participation}

New volunteers will move to the practical stage of their onboarding by actively participating in the organisation of ESN
Nicosia activities through Organising Committees (OCs).

For the first recruitment period, this stage will normally take place during October.

For the second recruitment period, this stage will normally take place during February.

During this stage, new volunteers are expected to join Organising Committees and contribute to the preparation and
implementation of activities.

This stage allows new volunteers to move from observing and attending activities to actively contributing to their
organisation. It also provides an opportunity to demonstrate responsibility, communication, teamwork and initiative
while gaining practical experience within the section.

## 3.6 Completion of Recruitment {#completion-of-recruitment}

The recruitment process is considered successfully completed when the new volunteer has fulfilled the onboarding
criteria, including:

* Attendance at a minimum of two out of the three mandatory training sessions.
* Successful participation in the September trial month.
* Respect for the rules and expectations applying during the trial period.
* Participation in Organising Committees during October.
* Demonstration of an appropriate level of involvement, cooperation and willingness to contribute to the section.

Upon successful completion of these criteria, the recruitment phase will be considered complete and the volunteer will
become an official member of ESN Cyprus.

The onboarding process is intended not only to assess participation, but also to ensure that new members have sufficient
time, guidance and practical experience to understand the organisation before officially becoming part of the section.
