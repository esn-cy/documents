# 1. Active Membership {#active-membership}

## 1.1 Definition of an Active Member {#definition-of-an-active-member}

An active member of ESN Cyprus is a volunteer who demonstrates consistent involvement in the section and contributes to
its activities, internal operations and community.

To be considered an Active Member, a member is expected to:

* Participate in at least two Organising Committees per month.
* Participate in Section Meetings.
* Participate in General Assemblies.
* Be part of at least one ESN Cyprus Team and actively contribute to its work.

These expectations are intended to establish a reasonable and transparent minimum level of participation for all
members. They should not be understood simply as requirements to be completed, but as opportunities for volunteers to
remain connected to the section and contribute meaningfully to its development.

## 1.2 Purpose of the Active Membership Criteria {#purpose-of-the-active-membership-criteria}

The Active Membership criteria aim to create a healthier and more balanced volunteering environment within ESN Cyprus.

By establishing common expectations, we aim to:

* Encourage members to remain motivated and involved throughout the mandate.
* Promote responsibility and ownership among volunteers.
* Distribute tasks and responsibilities more fairly across the section.
* Avoid situations where a small number of highly active members continuously carry the majority of the workload.
* Encourage members to gain experience through different activities, committees and Teams.
* Strengthen communication and collaboration between members.
* Increase participation in the democratic and decision-making processes of the section.
* Create greater transparency regarding what is expected from an active member.

Active membership should ultimately reflect a member's willingness to contribute to the community while also developing
personally and professionally through their involvement in ESN.
