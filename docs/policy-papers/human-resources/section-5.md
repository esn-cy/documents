# 5. Intervention Policy {#intervention-policy}

## 5.1 Support Before Intervention {#support-before-intervention}

The purpose of VPA intervention is not to immediately penalise members for periods of lower activity. Its purpose is
first to understand the situation and identify whether additional communication, guidance or support is needed.

If a member consistently does not meet the Active Membership criteria, VPA should approach the member privately and
respectfully.

The first step should be a conversation focused on understanding the reason for inactivity. The VPA and the member can
agree on realistic steps for improving their involvement.

## 5.2 One-Month Trial Period {#one-month-trial-period}

Following the initial VPA intervention, a member who expresses an interest in becoming active again will enter a
one-month trial period.

The purpose of this period is to provide the member with a fair opportunity to demonstrate renewed interest, commitment
and willingness to actively participate in the section.

During the trial period, the member will be expected to make a clear effort towards meeting the Active Membership
criteria, including participation in Organising Committees, Section Meetings, General Assemblies and their respective
Team.

The trial period should not be approached as a punishment, but rather as an opportunity for the member to reconnect with
the section, rediscover their motivation and demonstrate their intention to remain an active part of ESN Nicosia.

VPA should maintain communication with the member throughout this period and, where necessary, provide guidance
regarding opportunities for involvement. At the end of the one-month period, the member's participation and overall
engagement will be reviewed.

The evaluation should consider not only whether individual requirements were fulfilled, but also whether the member
demonstrated a genuine and consistent effort to become involved again.

If the member successfully demonstrates renewed engagement, they will continue their involvement as an Active Member.

## 5.3 Continued Inactivity {#continued-inactivity}

If, following the one-month trial period, the member continues to demonstrate inactivity or does not show reasonable
effort towards meeting the Active Membership criteria, VPA may proceed with further intervention in accordance with the
internal regulations and procedures of ESN Nicosia.

Any further intervention should be based on consistency, transparency and equal treatment. Similar situations should be
approached according to the same principles, while still allowing reasonable consideration of individual circumstances.

Before stronger measures are taken, the member should be informed about the outcome of their trial period and given
clear feedback regarding the reasons why their level of participation is considered insufficient.

The objective of the intervention process should always be to provide members with a reasonable opportunity to reconnect
with the section before stronger measures regarding their membership status are considered.

## 5.4 Individual Circumstances {#individual-circumstances}

This policy recognises that volunteering takes place alongside university, employment, family responsibilities and
personal commitments.

Members who know that they will temporarily be unable to meet the Active Membership criteria are encouraged to
communicate with the VPA in advance whenever possible.

Temporary periods of reduced participation should therefore be considered individually and should not automatically be
treated as a lack of commitment.

Open communication between members and VPA is essential for ensuring that the policy remains both fair and human
centred.
