# 4. Onboarding {#onboarding}

Once a recruit has successfully completed the recruitment process and becomes an official member of ESN Nicosia, they
must complete the official onboarding requirements of the section.

The onboarding process ensures that every new member has access to the necessary ESN platforms, communication channels
and resources required to participate fully in the section.

If any member refuses to complete the onboarding process they won't be able to continue being part of the Section.

## 4.1 ESN Galaxy Account {#esn-galaxy-account}

All new members are required to create an ESN Galaxy Account through the ESN Portal.

The Galaxy Account officially connects the member to the ESN network and provides access to platforms and opportunities
associated with their membership.

New members are responsible for creating their Galaxy Account within the timeframe communicated by the Board and
ensuring that their account is correctly set up.

## 4.2 ESN Google Account {#esn-google-account}

All new members are also required to apply for their ESN Google Account.

Through this account, each member will receive their personal ESN email address, which will be used for internal
communication and access to important information, resources and relevant ESN platforms.

Members are expected to regularly check their ESN email and use their account appropriately, as important information
concerning the section may be communicated through it.
