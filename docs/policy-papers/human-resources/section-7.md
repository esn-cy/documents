# 7. Building a Culture of Active Participation {#building-a-culture-of-active-participation}

The long-term purpose of this policy is not simply to distinguish between active and inactive members. It is to create
an organisational culture where volunteers want to participate, take responsibility and feel that their contribution
matters.

The VPA should therefore work together with the Board, Coordinators and members to identify the reasons behind
inactivity and continuously improve the volunteer experience.

Recognition, bonding opportunities, clear communication, meaningful responsibilities, accessible leadership
opportunities and a welcoming internal environment should complement the Active Membership criteria.

A strong section cannot be created through rules alone. It is created when members understand why their participation
matters, feel supported in taking responsibility and recognise that the effort they contribute is also being contributed
by the people around them.

Through this Active Membership and Intervention Policy, ESN Cyprus aims to establish clearer expectations while
maintaining the supportive and community-oriented character of the organisation.

Ultimately, our goal is to build a section where participation is encouraged, responsibilities are shared fairly,
members are supported, and every volunteer has the opportunity to contribute, develop and make an impact.
