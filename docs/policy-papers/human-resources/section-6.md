# 6. When to Contact VPA {#when-to-contact-vpa}

The Vice President for Administrator is available to support members in matters that concern their experience,
cooperation and well-being within ESN Nicosia. Members should feel comfortable approaching VPA when a situation within
the section affects their ability to participate, collaborate or feel respected and included.

Members are encouraged to contact VPA when:

* A conflict or recurring disagreement occurs between members of ESN Nicosia and cannot be resolved appropriately
  between the individuals involved.

* A member experiences behaviour within ESN activities, meetings, Teams, Organising Committees or internal communication
  channels that makes them feel uncomfortable, excluded or disrespected.

* A situation involving members begins to negatively affect teamwork, communication, participation or the overall
  environment of the section.

* A member requires support regarding their involvement, motivation, responsibilities or integration within ESN Nicosia.

* A member believes that an internal issue requires confidential guidance, mediation or intervention.

* A personal circumstance is directly affecting the member's ability to fulfil their ESN responsibilities, and they wish
  to communicate this to VPA.

VPA should primarily be involved in matters that have a clear connection to ESN Nicosia, its activities, internal
relationships or the functioning of the section.

Conflicts or disagreements between members that occur entirely outside the ESN environment and concern exclusively
personal matters should generally remain outside the responsibilities of the VPA. Members are encouraged to resolve
private disagreements independently and avoid involving the VPA solely because the individuals concerned are also
members of ESN Nicosia.

However, if an external or personal conflict begins to affect the members' behaviour, cooperation, participation or the
overall environment within ESN Nicosia, members may approach the VPA for support. In such circumstances, the VPA should
focus specifically on the impact of the situation within the section rather than attempting to resolve the members'
private disagreement itself.

The purpose of this distinction is to maintain appropriate boundaries regarding the role of the VPA while ensuring that
members have access to support whenever interpersonal situations begin to affect the functioning, safety or inclusivity
of the section.

Members approaching the VPA should do so respectfully and, where possible, provide a clear and factual description of
the situation. The VPA should handle concerns with discretion, impartiality and respect for the individuals involved,
while recognising that confidentiality may have reasonable limitations where further action is necessary.
