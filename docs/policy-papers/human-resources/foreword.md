# Foreword {#foreword}

As we begin the academic year 2026-2027, one of our main priorities within ESN Cyprus is to strengthen the involvement,
motivation and sense of responsibility of our volunteers while maintaining a fair, supportive and inclusive environment
for all members of the section.

ESN Cyprus is built on the commitment of its volunteers. Every activity, initiative, project and achievement of our
section depends on members who are willing to participate, collaborate, take responsibility and contribute to the
development of our organisation. For this reason, active membership should represent more than simply holding the status
of an ESN member; it should reflect meaningful and consistent participation in the life of the section.

The purpose of this Policy Paper is to establish a clear and transparent framework regarding Active Membership and VPA
Intervention. By defining common expectations for all members, we aim to encourage greater participation, strengthen
accountability and create a fairer environment in which responsibilities are shared across the network rather than
continuously relying on the same group of volunteers.

At the same time, this policy is not intended to create unnecessary pressure or to punish members who may temporarily be
less active. Academic responsibilities, employment, personal circumstances and other commitments can naturally affect a
volunteer's availability. Therefore, the role of VPA should not only be to monitor participation, but also to communicate
with members, understand potential difficulties and provide support when needed.

Through this approach, we aim to motivate members to take initiative, explore different areas of ESN, develop new skills
and become more connected with the section. Participation in Organising Committees, Teams, Section Meetings and General
Assemblies provides members with opportunities to understand how the organisation functions, contribute to
decision-making and take ownership of the community they are part of.

Above all, this policy aims to promote a culture of participation, responsibility, fairness and mutual support. Every
active member contributes differently, but maintaining a strong volunteer network requires a shared minimum level of
involvement and commitment from everyone.

The Active Membership and Intervention Policy therefore provides a common framework through which expectations can be
communicated transparently, participation can be encouraged consistently and VPA interventions can be approached
constructively and fairly.
