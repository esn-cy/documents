# 2. Participation and Responsibility {#participation-and-responsibility}

## 2.1 Organising Committees {#organising-committees}

Members are expected to participate in at least two Organising Committees per month.

Organising Committees are an important part of volunteer development because they allow members to take responsibility
for the preparation and implementation of activities rather than participating only as attendees.

Through Organising Committees, members can develop practical skills such as teamwork, communication, event planning,
problem-solving, leadership and time management while directly contributing to the work of the section.

## 2.2 Section Meetings {#section-meetings}

Members are expected to participate regularly in Section Meetings.

Section Meetings are an important space for internal communication, updates, discussion and coordination. Regular
participation ensures that members remain informed about what is happening within ESN Cyprus and have the opportunity to
express their opinions, propose ideas and contribute to discussions concerning the section.

## 2.3 General Assemblies {#general-assemblies}

Members are expected to participate in General Assemblies.

General Assemblies represent an essential part of the democratic structure of the organisation. Participation allows
members to remain informed about important developments, understand decisions affecting the section and actively
contribute to its direction.

## 2.4 Team Involvement {#team-involvement}

Every active member is expected to be part of at least one Team within ESN Cyprus and contribute to its work.

Teams provide members with the opportunity to focus on areas that match their interests and skills while taking greater
ownership of specific aspects of the organisation.

Team participation should encourage members not only to complete assigned tasks, but also to propose ideas, collaborate
with others and gradually take greater initiative within the section.
