# 2. Communication Boundaries & Workflows {#communication-boundaries-workflows}

To maintain organisational discipline, ensure timely communication, and protect volunteer well-being, all material
submissions, review requests, and communication-related check-ups must take place within designated operational windows
and follow the established submission timelines.

# 2.1 Operational Review Hours {#operational-review-hours}

Submissions, design drafts, and post reviews sent to [communication@esncy.org](mailto:communication@esncy.org) and
to [vice-communication@esncy.org](mailto:vice-communication@esncy.org) which are processed strictly during the following
operational windows (Cyprus Time - EEST):

* **Afternoon Window:** 12:00 - 14:00
* **Evening Window:** 18:00 - 20:00

*Note: Any submission or request received outside of these specified hours will be officially logged as received at the
start of the next valid review window.*

## 2.2 Submission Deadlines {#submission-deadlines}

### 2.2.1 Ordinary & Weekly Events {#ordinary-weekly-events}

For all regular weekly events, the Head of the Organising Committee (OC) is responsible for submitting the final event
description and cover to the Vice Communication Manager (VCM) and Communication Manager (CM)  by Friday of the week
preceding the event.

Failure to submit the required materials by the Friday deadline may result in the Vice Communication Manager and/or
Communication Manager completing or adapting the necessary communication materials directly to ensure that the event is
properly promoted and deadlines are met.

### 2.2.2 Social Impact Days {#social-impact-days}

Campaigns organised under Social Impact initiatives follow the 7-day advance submission workflow. The Social Impact
Coordinator (or acting Coordinator) is responsible for obtaining the official promotional files and communication
materials from ESN International and providing them to the Communication Team within the same 7-day advance timeframe.

### 2.2.3 Trips & High-Complexity Events {#trips-high-complexity-events}

Trips and other high-complexity or multi-faceted events require structured, long-term promotional timelines to ensure
that participants receive all necessary information clearly and in a timely manner. Content and promotional materials
should be prepared and submitted according to the following schedule:

**Save the Date — 6 Weeks / 42 Days Before**

* Teaser graphic
* Tentative dates
* Destination highlight

**Full Event Announcement — 4 Weeks / 28 Days Before**

* Comprehensive event description
* Pricing tiers
* Registration link
* Key event details

**Trip Information Suite — 1 Week / 7 Days Before**

* Meet the Team
* Trip Schedule
* Survival Guide, including packing and logistical advice
* Location Guides, including venues and key spots
* Safety Tips & Emergency Protocols
* Code of Conduct
