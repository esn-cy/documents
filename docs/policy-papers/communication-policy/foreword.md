# Foreword {#foreword}

Dear ESN Cyprus Network,

As we begin the 2026-2027 mandate, effective communication is an essential part of how ESN Cyprus connects with
students, volunteers, partners and the wider community.

As our communication continues to grow across different platforms, campaigns and types of content, having a clear and
shared system becomes increasingly important. This Policy Paper has been developed to establish that system: to clarify
responsibilities, define workflows, maintain our visual identity, ensure timely communication, and provide common
standards for everyone involved in communication.

The purpose of this document is not to limit creativity or initiative, but to make our work more organised, consistent
and sustainable. It provides guidance for both current and future Communication Team members, helping us maintain
continuity even as volunteers and responsibilities change.

Most importantly, this policy aims to make communication a shared responsibility while ensuring that everyone knows what
is expected, who is responsible, when something needs to be done, and how our communication should represent ESN Cyprus.

I hope this Policy Paper will serve as a practical reference throughout the year and help us build a communication
system that is clear, professional, sustainable and, above all, shaped by our volunteers.

Kindly,

Milana Babiak

Communication Manager of ESN Cyprus for 2026-2027
