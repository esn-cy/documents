# 5. AI Usage {#ai-usage}

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools may be used by the ESN Cyprus Communication Team exclusively for proofreading
publicly available information and text intended for public communication. Their use is limited to improving the
grammar, spelling, clarity, and readability of such content. AI tools must not be used to generate original content,
develop creative concepts, or process internal, confidential, or personal information.

## 5.1 Permitted Use {#permitted-use}

AI tools may be used to support public communication through content drafting, proofreading, translation, adaptation,
planning, research, workflow organisation, and accessibility improvements. Their use must be limited to publicly
available information. Confidential, personal, or sensitive information must never be entered into AI tools.

## 5.2 Human Oversight & Accountability {#human-oversight-accountability}

AI tools must be treated as supportive resources rather than substitutes for human judgement, creativity, or
responsibility.

* All AI-assisted content must be reviewed by the responsible team member before submission.

* The responsible team member remains accountable for the accuracy, appropriateness, originality, and quality of the
  submitted material, regardless of whether AI tools were used.

* AI-generated information must be checked for factual accuracy, particularly when communicating event details, dates,
  prices, registration procedures, or other information relevant to participants.

The use of AI does not exempt any material from the submission, review, and approval requirements established in this
Policy Paper.

## 5.3 Data Protection & Confidentiality {#data-protection-confidentiality}

AI tools must be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable GDPR and data-protection requirements.

* Personal data, confidential organisational information, internal documents, private conversations, or other sensitive
  material must not be entered into AI tools unless the use has been appropriately authorised and complies with
  applicable data-protection requirements.

* Photographs, videos, or other media containing identifiable individuals must not be uploaded to AI platforms for
  processing, modification, or generation without the appropriate authorisation and consideration of the individuals'
  rights.

* Team members must exercise caution when using external AI platforms and ensure that their use does not compromise the
  privacy of volunteers, Erasmus students, partners, or other individuals.

## 5.4 Transparency & Appropriate Use {#transparency-appropriate-use}

Where AI has made a substantial contribution to a published communication material, the Communication Manager may
require the responsible team member to disclose its use, particularly where transparency is necessary to maintain trust
or clarify the nature of the content.

AI-generated content must not be presented as an authentic testimonial, statement, or experience of a real individual
when it is not.

The Communication Manager, in coordination with the Vice Communication Manager if appointed, retains the authority to
request revisions, reject AI-assisted materials, or restrict the use of particular AI tools where necessary to protect
ESN Cyprus's standards, reputation, and responsibilities.
