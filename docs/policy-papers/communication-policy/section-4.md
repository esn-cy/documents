# 4. Media Usage & Data Privacy {#media-usage-data-privacy}

## 4.1 Internal ESN Usage {#internal-esn-usage}

Photos and videos captured by Organising Committees or designated ESN photographers during official ESN Cyprus events
are intended for official ESN Cyprus promotional, archival, reporting, and organisational purposes.

Such content may be used across official ESN Cyprus communication channels and internal ESN materials, provided that it
is handled in accordance with applicable GDPR and data-protection requirements and the purpose for which the content was
collected.

ESN volunteers and photographers must ensure that media containing identifiable individuals is used responsibly and only
for legitimate ESN-related purposes.

## 4.2 Private & External Usage {#private-external-usage}

Photos and videos captured during official ESN Cyprus activities must not be used for personal, private, commercial, or
non-ESN purposes without the appropriate permission from the identifiable individuals featured in the content.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Personal social media accounts
* Personal portfolios or websites
* Professional or academic portfolios
* Personal promotional materials
* External organisations or projects
* Commercial use
* Any other non-ESN publication or distribution

Permission to capture or use media for official ESN Cyprus purposes does not automatically grant permission for personal
or external use.

Where consent or other appropriate legal authorisation is required, the content must not be published or otherwise used
until the necessary approval has been obtained. For content where individual faces are clearly identifiable, explicit
written consent from the relevant individuals should be obtained before using the content for personal or external
purposes, particularly where the use goes beyond the original ESN-related purpose.

## 4.3 External Photo Sharing & Shared Drive {#external-photo-sharing-shared-drive}

For selected activities, including:

* Ithaki;
* Trips;
* Boat Parties;
* And major ESN Cyprus events.

Photographs may be made available to participants through a designated external shared drive. The purpose of this drive
is to allow participants to access and download photographs from the activities they attended.

The following rules apply:

* Only photo/video material selected and approved by the ESN Cyprus Communication Team may be uploaded to the shared
  drive.

* ESN Cyprus volunteers may download photo/video material from the shared drive for personal use, but this does not
  automatically grant permission to publish or use it for commercial or other external purposes.

* ESN volunteers must respect the privacy of individuals appearing in the photo/video material and avoid using or
  sharing it in ways that may violate their rights.

* Photo/video material must not be distributed, or used for other purposes without the appropriate permission, where
  required.

* The shared drive must be managed responsibly, and its access link should be distributed through official ESN Cyprus
  communication channels.

### 4.3.1 Download Period & Removal of Materials {#download-period-removal-of-materials}

The Organising Committee (OC) responsible for each event must upload the approved photo/video material to the designated
external shared drive. The upload date is defined as the calendar date on which the OC uploads the material and makes it
available in the drive.

Photo/video material will remain available for 14 days from the upload date.

The Organising Committee (OC) responsible for the event must clearly communicate the availability of the shared drive
and the download deadline to participants. Participants are responsible for downloading any photo/video material they
wish to keep before the deadline.

After the 14-day period has ended, the photo/video material must be removed from the shared drive in accordance with ESN
Cyprus's data-protection procedures.

The removal of photo/video material from the shared drive does not automatically grant permission for its continued use
elsewhere. Any further use must comply with applicable permissions, legal requirements, and the purpose for which the
material was originally collected.

## 4.4 Photography Preferences & Individual Requests {#photography-preferences-individual-requests}

ESN Cyprus will provide an online form through which individuals can communicate their photography preferences and
request the removal or modification of photographs in which they appear. The form will be accessible 24 hours a day,
allowing individuals to submit requests at any time.

The form will collect the following information:

* **Name:** To identify the individual submitting the request.
* **Email address:** To allow the Communication Team to contact the individual regarding their request.
* **Photograph or relevant event information:** To help identify the photographs or videos concerned.
* **Photography preference:** To indicate how the individual wishes their image to be handled.

Individuals may use the form to:

* Request not to appear in photographs or videos taken during ESN Cyprus activities.

* Request that their face be blurred in photographs or videos where they are identifiable.

* Request the removal of photographs or videos in which they appear from ESN Cyprus communication channels or shared
  materials, where applicable.

The Communication Team must review all submitted requests and take appropriate action. For future events, the relevant
Organising Committee and photographers must be informed of the individual's preferences. For existing photos/videos, the
Communication Team must assess the request and, where appropriate, remove the content, blur the individual's face, or
restrict further distribution. Submitting the form does not replace any consent or other legal authorisation required
under applicable data-protection laws.

## 4.5 Responsibilities of the Communication Team & Organising Committees {#responsibilities-of-the-communication-team-organising-committees}

The Communication Team and Organising Committees share responsibility for ensuring that event photography and
videography are handled appropriately.

Their responsibilities include:

* Informing participants about photography and the intended use of event media.
* Ensuring that photographers and relevant volunteers are aware of ESN Cyprus's media and data-protection procedures.
* Communicating the availability of shared photographs and the applicable download deadline.
* Ensuring that photography preferences and individual requests are communicated to the relevant team members.
* Reviewing and addressing requests concerning the use, modification, or removal of photographs.
* Ensuring that media is stored, shared, and retained appropriately.

The Communication Manager, in coordination with the Vice Communication Manager if appointed, oversees the implementation
of these procedures within the Communication Team.

## 4.6 Data Protection & Responsible Handling {#data-protection-responsible-handling}

All personal data collected through photography, videography, shared drives, or photography-preference forms must be
handled responsibly and in accordance with applicable GDPR and data-protection requirements.

Access to photographs, videos, and personal information must be limited to individuals who require it for legitimate ESN
Cyprus responsibilities.

Personal information collected through the photography-preference form must only be used for the purpose of managing the
individual's request and related communication. It must not be used for unrelated purposes.

ESN Cyprus must ensure that personal data is not retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was
collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Individuals must be provided with appropriate information about the processing of their personal data, including the
relevant purposes, legal basis, retention arrangements, and how they may exercise their data-protection rights.

Any concerns regarding the handling of photographs, videos, or personal data should be communicated to the Communication
Manager or the appropriate ESN Cyprus representative for review and follow-up.
