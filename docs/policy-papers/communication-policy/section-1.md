# 1. Scope & Brand Identity {#scope-brand-identity}

To maintain visual consistency and protect the professional reputation of ESN Cyprus, all official communication and
media must follow the established digital channels, branding standards, and communication procedures.

## 1.1 Official Channels & Access Control {#official-channels-access-control}

The Communication Team is responsible for managing all official ESN Cyprus communication platforms and media assets.
Access, platform administration, and team responsibilities are organised as follows:

* **Communication Manager:** Holds overall responsibility for and administrative oversight of all communication
  platforms and media assets.

* **Communication Manager Board Supporters:** Support the Communication Manager, coordinate day-to-day platform access
  and operations, and ensure assigned tasks are completed efficiently.

* **Communication Team:** Creates the content in accordance with the Communication Team's guidelines and established
  workflows.

## 1.2 Platforms & Assets Covered {#platforms-assets-covered}

**Social Media:**  Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok.

**Direct Messaging & Web:** ESN Cyprus official website and official WhatsApp channels/groups.

**Media Assets:** Merchandise designs, event photography and videography, press releases, promotional materials, and
printed collateral.

## 1.3 Branding Standards {#branding-standards}

All visual and written content produced for or on behalf of ESN Cyprus must follow the **ESN International Visual
Identity Manual (VIM)** and the **ESN Cyprus Communication Guidelines**, ensuring consistency in branding, tone of
voice, and professional communication.

* **ESN International VIM:** Covers official fonts, colours, logos, layouts, and international campaigns.

* **ESN Cyprus Communication Guidelines:** Covers local templates, tone of voice, messaging, and communication
  procedures.

## 1.4 Workflow & Deadlines {#workflow-deadlines}

* All work must be submitted to the Communication Manager and Vice Communication Manager **at least 72 hours before the
  final deadline via email**.

* Feedback, approval, or requested changes will be provided **within 24 hours** of submission via email.

* If changes are requested, the responsible team member has **24 hours to update and resubmit the work via email**.

* If the work is not updated within this timeframe, the **Communication Manager or Vice Communication Manager may make
  the necessary changes directly** to ensure the deadline and communication standards are met.
