# 3. Editorial Control & Compliance {#editorial-control-compliance}

## 3.1 Approval Authority {#approval-authority}

Final authority for the publication of any graphic, caption, video, press release, or public statement rests exclusively
with the Communication Manager ([communication@esncy.org](mailto:communication@esncy.org)), in coordination with the
Vice Communication Manager ([vice-communication@esncy.org](mailto:vice-communication@esncy.org)), if appointed. No
material may be published on public channels without explicit sign-off.

## 3.2 Correction Window & Non-Compliance {#correction-window-non-compliance}

Content that fails to incorporate requested edits within the 3-day window **will be withheld from publication**. The
responsible OC assumes full accountability for any reduced event turnout or delayed promotion resulting from missed
deadlines.
