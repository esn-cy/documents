# Product Sales and Ticketing {#product-sales-ticketing}

To ensure financial transparency, security, and accurate record-keeping, strict protocols govern all incoming funds from
event tickets, product sales, and participation fees.

## Cashless Operations {#cashless-operations}

ESN Cyprus operates on a strictly cashless basis. All in-person transactions must be processed securely using the JCC
SoftPOS application on Android devices. Cash payments are not accepted.

## Mandatory Receipts {#mandatory-receipts}

Transparency is mandatory. Whenever money flows into ESN Cyprus, regardless of the amount or the item being sold, an
official receipt must be issued to the purchaser immediately.

## Personal Accounts {#personal-accounts}

Under no circumstances may a volunteer accept payments for ESN Cyprus products, trips, or services into their personal
bank accounts, personal digital wallets, or third-party payment apps (e.g., Revolut, PayPal). All funds must be routed
directly into official ESN Cyprus accounts.

## Single-Vendor Events {#single-vendor-events}

For events where the sole expense is tied directly to one venue or vendor (e.g., purchasing drinks at a Beer Pong
tournament), participants must pay the venue directly. ESN Cyprus will not act as a financial middleman to collect and
transfer these specific funds.

## Trip Ticketing {#trip-ticketing}

For safety and capacity management, all ESN Cyprus trips require access control through the use of tickets and
wristbands.

The majority of our sales are conducted through our official online ticketing platform.

When in-person sales occur, they must be manually registered onto the same online ticketing platform at the moment of
the transaction. This guarantees procedural consistency, ensures accurate participant lists, and requires a separate
receipt to be issued.

For our main trips 3 ticket prices should be established:

* ESNcard Price: The discounted price for ESNcard holders.
* ESN Cyprus Pass Price: The baseline price of the ticket that will be awarded to pass holders.
* Standard Price: A marked up price from the baseline for people without a pass.

This measure shall be put in place to deter non-mobility participants from buying tickets as our ticketing is online and
open to everyone.
