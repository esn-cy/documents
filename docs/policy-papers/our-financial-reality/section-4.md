# Event Planning and Budgeting {#event-planning-budgeting}

To maintain the financial stability of ESN Cyprus, all events must strictly adhere to predefined budget limitations and
approval workflows based on their scale and categorisation.

## Ordinary Events {#ordinary-events}

Typical weekly events operate within a maximum budget cap established in the annual ESN Cyprus budget.

Ordinary events that require funding beyond the standard weekly cap require explicit, written consent from the Treasurer
prior to any commitment of funds.

Event organisers must communicate their requested material needs to the Treasurer at least 72 hours before making any
purchases. This ensures the purchases are genuinely necessary and prevents spending funds on items already available in
the office inventory.

## Erasmus Wednesdays at Ithaki {#erasmus-wednesdays-at-ithaki}

Because these events generate consistent revenue for the network, they are allocated a dedicated, increased slush fund
in the annual budget.

The Head of the Ithaki OC has the authority to dictate budgetary allocations for each individual Wednesday event at
their discretion. However, the Head must ensure that the sum of all individual event expenses never exceeds the total
allocated slush fund.

## Trips and High-Complexity Events {#trips-high-complexity-events}

A "Trip" is strictly defined as any event that has a built-in profit margin, *or* any event that includes transportation
plus at least one additional expense (regardless of whether it generates a profit).

The planning and financial structuring of a trip must follow this sequential order:

1. The Organising Committee (OC) holds preliminary meetings to define the scope and draft a schedule.
2. The OC refines the draft schedule in coordination with the Vice President for Coordination (VPC).
3. The OC holds a mandatory meeting with the Treasurer to build the official trip budget.

Each trip's minimum expected profit is defined in the annual budget. The detailed trip budget formulated by the OC and
Treasurer must be built specifically to guarantee that profit target is met.

The trip budget must be completely finalised and locked no later than one week (7 days) before the departure date.

Immediately following the trip, a mandatory meeting must be held between the Head of the OC and the Treasurer. The
purpose of this meeting is to fully reconcile all generated incomes against incurred expenses. This reconciliation is
generally tracked using a standard template, though the Treasurer may establish their own standardised reporting method.
