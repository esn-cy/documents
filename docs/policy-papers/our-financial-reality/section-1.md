# Reimbursements {#reimbursements}

While our work is strictly voluntary, we firmly believe that no member should bear a personal financial burden while
carrying out authorised duties for the network. This policy outlines the framework to ensure that all legitimate,
approved expenses incurred on behalf of ESN Cyprus are promptly and fairly reimbursed.

## Eligibility {#eligibility}

Reimbursements are available to the following individuals, provided the expenses are linked to official ESN activities:

* The ESN Cyprus Board and Board Supporters
* Official Teams and Working Groups
* Active Members that have received prior approval from the Treasurer

## Applicable Expenses {#applicable-expenses}

As a general rule, all expenses must be pre-approved unless an explicit exception (e.g., an unforeseen emergency) has
been granted. Every expense must align with ESN Cyprus's core values and fall within the current budgetary provisions.

Eligible expenses include, but are not limited to, the following categories.

### Event/Trip Expenses {#event-trip-expenses}

Necessary costs for local events, including event supplies, venue rentals, decorations, food, beverages, and fuel for
designated safety cars.

### International Event Expenses {#international-event-expenses}

Participation fees and related delegation expenses for official ESN International events. Eligible events include:

* General Assemblies (GA)
* Erasmus Generation Meeting (EGM)
* National Boards Training (NBT)
* Regional Platforms
* Eduk8 Training Events

Delegation expenses shall also be covered for these events based on the annual budget. These are costs associated with
officially representing ESN Cyprus at the international events listed above. These may include promotional material to
give away and food for international dinners.

Please note that expenses and participation fees for National Events, such as National Assemblies (NAs), are strictly
not covered by ESN Cyprus. These costs remain the responsibility of the individual volunteer or their respective local
section.

### Office Supplies & Administration {#office-supplies-administration}

Essential materials for daily operations, such as stationery, printing costs, and basic office supplies.

## Procedure {#procedure}

To ensure transparency and proper financial tracking, the following procedure applies to all reimbursement requests:

* **Formal Submission:** A formal request must be submitted directly to the Treasurer. This request must clearly explain
  the reason and organisational purpose of the expense.

* **Standardised Format:** Requests must be submitted using the standardised method established by the Treasurer.

* **Contingency:** In the prolonged absence of the Treasurer, reimbursement requests should be directed to the President
  of ESN Cyprus.

## Limitations & Compliance {#limitations-compliance}

To maintain the financial integrity of ESN Cyprus, the following limitations are strictly enforced:

* **Proof of Payment:** A valid, legal receipt or official proof of payment **must** accompany every request. Any
  request lacking proper documentation will be automatically denied.

* **Deadline:** All reimbursement requests must be submitted within **7 days** of the original payment date. Late
  submissions risk not being processed.

* **Exclusions:** Personal expenses, unapproved costs, or purchases made outside the scope of authorized ESN activities
  will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Returns and Refunds {#returns-refunds}

If an item or service for which you have already been reimbursed by ESN Cyprus is subsequently returned, cancelled, or
refunded by the vendor, you are required to return those funds to the organisation.

The equivalent funds must be transferred directly back to the official ESN Cyprus bank account within 7 days of
receiving the refund from the vendor.

You must promptly notify the Treasurer of the return or cancellation and provide any relevant proof of the refund
transaction to ensure the budget is properly reconciled.
