## **ARTICLE 2: PAYMENT TERMS AND OPTIONS**

2.1 Mandatory Documentation: No payment shall be processed without the prior submission of a valid, legal receipt or
formal invoice provided by the Merchant.

2.2 Payment Methods: ESN Cyprus shall settle the agreed amount using one of the following payment methods, subject to
the respective notice periods:

* Cash: Requires a minimum of fourteen (14) days advance notice from the Merchant.

* Card: Requires a minimum of seven (7) days advance notice from the Merchant.

* Bank Transfer: Requires no advance notice. Processing time for bank transfers is estimated at 3 to 5 business days
  following the receipt of the mandatory invoice.

## **ARTICLE 3: CONFIDENTIALITY**

3.1 Confidential Information: Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of any proprietary or sensitive
information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement ("Confidential Information"). Neither Party
shall disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the disclosing Party,
except as required by law.

3.2 Exclusions: Confidential Information does not include information that is:

1. publicly known at the time of disclosure,
2. becomes publicly known through no fault of the receiving Party,
3. already known to the receiving Party prior to disclosure, or
4. independently developed by the receiving Party.

## **ARTICLE 4: TERM AND TERMINATION**

4.1 Term: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until the delivery of goods/services and
successful payment, or until terminated. 

4.2 Termination: Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing a minimum of six (6) weeks' prior written
notice. Termination can also occur immediately upon material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach
within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

## **ARTICLE 5: MISCELLANEOUS**

5.1 Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of
Cyprus.

5.2 Amendments: No amendment or modification to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by
authorised representatives of both Parties.

5.3 Entire Agreement: This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject
hereof.
