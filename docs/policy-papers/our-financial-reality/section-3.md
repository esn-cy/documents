# Payments and Vendor Agreements {#payments-vendor-agreements}

To standardise outbound cash flow, ensure accountability, and protect ESN Cyprus from vendor disputes, the following
rules apply to all external payments and contracts.

## Merchant Agreements {#merchant-agreements}

A formal Merchant Payment Agreement must be executed for any vendor transaction that exceeds €100, or represents more
than 20% of a specific trip's total budget.

Only the President or the Treasurer is authorised to sign Merchant Payment Agreements on behalf of ESN Cyprus.
Volunteers cannot sign contracts on behalf of the organisation.

This contract can be found in Appendix A1.

## **Payment Methods & Timelines** {#payment-methods-timelines}

As outlined in our standard agreement, vendors must be informed of our payment options and notice periods.

### Bank Transfers {#bank-transfers}

The standard and preferred method. Processing takes 3 to 5 business days following the receipt of the mandatory invoice.
No advance notice from the vendor is required.

### Card Payments {#card-payments}

Executed using the physical section card. Vendors requesting card payments must provide a minimum of 7 days advance
notice.

### Cash Payments {#cash-payments}

While ESN Cyprus is cashless for incoming funds, outgoing cash payments to vendors are permitted via ATM withdrawals
(subject to a €2,000 daily limit). Vendors must provide a minimum of 14 days advance notice to accommodate this.

## Documentation & Compliance {#documentation-compliance}

No funds will be released, whether via bank transfer, card, or cash withdrawal, without the prior submission of a valid,
legal receipt or formal invoice from the merchant.

Immediately following a successful transaction, the responsible volunteer must upload the receipt or invoice to the
designated internal form for processing and financial reconciliation.

## Delivery Deadlines & Cancellations {#delivery-deadlines-cancellations}

### Late Delivery Cutoffs {#late-delivery-cutoffs}

When drafting the agreement, OC members must establish an absolute deadline for the delivery of physical goods. This
cutoff can be set prior to the event or on the day of, depending on logistical convenience. If goods are not delivered
by this cutoff date, ESN Cyprus holds the right to refuse the goods, cancel the order, and withhold payment without
penalty.

### Contract Termination {#contract-termination}

If an agreement needs to be terminated, a minimum of 6 weeks' prior written notice must be provided to the vendor,
allowing sufficient time to secure a replacement. Contracts may also be terminated immediately if a vendor fails to cure
a material breach within 14 days of notice.
