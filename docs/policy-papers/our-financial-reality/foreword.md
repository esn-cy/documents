# Foreword {#foreword}

Dear ESNers,

At the heart of ESN Cyprus is a profound commitment to enriching the international student experience. Everything we
achieve, from unforgettable trips across the island to our weekly cultural events, is entirely fueled by the passion,
time, and boundless energy of our volunteers. You are the driving force of this network.

However, behind every great event is a foundation of planning, logistics, and financial management. Historically, the
financial side of our operations has sometimes felt like a "black box". It is like a complex mix of budgets, receipts,
and procedures that can seem intimidating or disconnected from the actual joy of organizing. We recognize that there has
often been a gap in knowledge regarding how our section’s finances actually work, leading to uncertainty and unnecessary
stress for our event organisers.

This document was created to bridge that gap.

Our goal is complete financial transparency. We want to demystify the budgeting process, clarify exactly how money flows
in and out of the organisation, and ensure that you never have to second-guess the procedures for ticketing, vendor
agreements, or getting reimbursed for your hard work.

When you have a clear understanding of our financial guidelines, you are no longer just an event helper; you become an
empowered stakeholder in the organisation. By engaging more deeply with the financial realities of our section, you can
plan more creative events, negotiate better deals, and confidently manage resources without the fear of making a
procedural misstep.

Ultimately, these rules are not here to create red tape; they are here to protect you as volunteers, to protect the
network's sustainability, and to ensure that every single euro we raise is responsibly reinvested into making the
Erasmus generation's experience in Cyprus unforgettable.

Thank you for your dedication, your accountability, and your continuous hard work.

Yours,

Andreas Michael

Treasurer, ESN Cyprus
