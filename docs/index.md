# ESN Cyprus Legal Documents

Welcome to the official legal documents repository of **Erasmus Student Network Cyprus (ESN Cyprus)**.

This platform serves as our centralised, searchable, and version-controlled home for all statutory and regulatory texts
governing the organisation. It provides volunteers, section boards, the National Board, and external partners with
direct, transparent access to up-to-date legal frameworks.

---

## Document Directory

Our documentation is structured hierarchically. The repository is rolling out in scheduled stages:

### :lucide-badge-check: 1. Statutes (as of GA November 2025)

Republic of Cyprus. They define our legal identity, objectives, membership categories, administrative bodies, and

### :lucide-badge-check: 2. Code of Conduct (as of eGA June 2024)

The **Code of Conduct** sets the behavioural expectations, ethical standards, safety principles, and dispute-resolution
mechanisms across all activities, events, and communication channels connected with ESN Cyprus. Endorsing the standards
of ESN International, it guarantees a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for all volunteers, students, and
third parties.

* [:lucide-file-text: Table of Contents](code-of-conduct/table-of-contents.md)
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 1 – Preamble](code-of-conduct/article-1.md) *(Dignity, non-discrimination,
  diversity, and endorsement of ESN International standards)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 2 – Scope of Application](code-of-conduct/article-2.md) *(Application to
  volunteers, students, physical/online events, and external representation)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 3 – Behaviours](code-of-conduct/article-3.md) *(Anti-discrimination standards and
  strict prohibition of bullying, harassment, violence, and defamation)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 4 – Procedures](code-of-conduct/article-4.md) *(Violation reporting, on-site
  Points of Contact, reporting form, anti-retaliation, and case inquiries)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 5 – Sanctions](code-of-conduct/article-5.md) *(Sanction scales, proportionality
  criteria, National Board competence, and rights to appeal)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Article 6 – General Notes](code-of-conduct/article-6.md) *(Interim arbitration authority
  of the National Board and alignment with the Statutes)*

### 4. Policy Papers

**Policy Papers**  define the operational rules and standard operating procedures governing internal workflows
across ESN Cyprus. Unlike constitutional texts, these documents provide practical, everyday guidance, operational
thresholds, and standardised templates for volunteers.

#### :lucide-badge-check: Our Financial Reality (as of NA July 2026)

Authored by the Treasurer of ESN Cyprus, this paper demystifies the network's financial management, establishing
transparent and accountable rules for budgeting, reimbursements, ticketing, vendor contracts, and event cash flows.

* [:lucide-file-text: Table of Contents](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/table-of-contents.md)
* [:lucide-book-open: Foreword](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/foreword.md) *(Introductory note by Andreas Michael,
  Treasurer of ESN Cyprus)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Section 1 – Reimbursements](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/section-1.md) *(
  Eligibility, local vs. international event expenses, 7-day submission deadlines, and vendor refunds)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Section 2 – Product Sales and Ticketing](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/section-2.md)
  *(Cashless operations via JCC SoftPOS, mandatory receipts, strict prohibition of personal accounts, and tiered trip
  ticketing)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Section 3 – Payments and Vendor Agreements](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/section-3.md)
  *(Merchant agreements over €100, bank/card/cash payment timelines, delivery cutoffs, and cancellation rules)*
* [:lucide-circle-arrow-right: Section 4 – Event Planning and Budgeting](policy-papers/our-financial-reality/section-4.md)

## Governance & Updates

All legal documents published here reflect decisions approved by the **General Assembly of ESN Cyprus**. Any proposed
amendments to the text must undergo the formal statutory procedure outlined
in [Chapter H of the Statutes](statutes/chapter-h.md) prior to publication.
