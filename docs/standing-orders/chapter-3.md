# Chapter III: Board Supporters {#board-supporters}

## Article 5: Definition and Duties {#definition-and-duties}

* 5.1. Board Supporters are specific positions created to assist Board Members in their duties.

* 5.2. Specific descriptions and duties for each Supporter position must be documented and made available prior to the
  opening of applications.

* 5.3. For the purpose of ensuring effectiveness and functionality, the National Board may establish Board Supporter
  positions in any number and with responsibilities it considers appropriate, provided that such positions and their
  functioning remain in adherence to the practices and governing principles of ESN AISBL.

## Article 6: Appointment and Interim Process {#appointment-and-interim-process}

* 6.1. Application: Candidates for Board Supporter positions shall submit a formal written application to the National
  Board. The application must include a motivational letter and any additional requirements established by the
  respective National Board Member whom the Supporter will assist. Requirements must be objective, transparent, and
  communicated publicly during the call for applications.

* 6.2. Interim Appointment: Following an open review of applications, the National Board may appoint Board Supporters
  for an interim trial period based on merit, motivation, and compatibility with the role. The selection process shall
  be conducted in good faith and with equal opportunity principles.

* 6.3. Trial Period: The trial period shall run from the interim appointment until the next General Assembly (please
  refer to Chapter III). During this period, the Board Supporter and the responsible National Board Member shall
  collaborate in order to evaluate working compatibility and role suitability. A Board Supporter may voluntarily
  withdraw from the position at any time during the trial period.

* 6.4. Approval by the General Assembly: At the first General Assembly following the appointment, the responsible
  National Board Member and the Board Supporter shall present a report on the collaboration and performance. The General
  Assembly shall vote on whether to approve the appointment for the remainder of the mandate.

    * 6.4.1. Any candidate board supporter must not hold or be a candidate for another position within the section.

* 6.5. Approval shall be decided by simple majority. Voting options shall be *Yes*, *No*, or *Abstain*.

* 6.6. Recruitment Timelines

    * 6.6.1. The first annual recruitment period for Board Supporters is mandatory and shall follow this framework:
        * Call for Supporters: August
        * Interim Appointment: Late August
        * Trial Period: September - October
        * Approval Vote: October General Assembly
    * 6.6.1. The second annual recruitment period for Board Supporters is optional and shall follow this framework:
        * Call for Supporters: December
        * Interim Appointment: Early January
        * Trial Period: January
        * Approval Vote: February General Assembly

## Article 7: Removal from Position {#removal-from-position}

* 7.1. The removal of a Board Supporter shall follow the procedure below:

    * 7.1.1. A proposal for the removal of a Board Supporter may be submitted by the National Board Member responsible
      or by one-third (1/3) of the active members.
    * 7.1.2. The Board Supporter shall be invited to provide explanations at the next Regular General Assembly. If at
      least two-thirds (2/3) of the Regular General Assembly vote in favour of removal by absolute majority, the
      individual shall be removed from their Board Supporter position, without losing their status as a member of the
      organisation.

* 7.2. Grounds for removal of a Board Supporter include:

    * 7.2.1. Inactivity in the role or conduct incompatible with the duties of the role,
    * 7.2.2. Unjustified absence from two (2) Regular or Extraordinary General Assemblies;
    * 7.2.3. Conviction by a court of law for any criminal offence;
    * 7.2.4. Obtaining personal financial benefit from the activities or assets of the organisation;
    * 7.2.5. Failure to comply with decisions of the General Assembly;
    * 7.2.6. Violation of the organisation's Code of Conduct.
