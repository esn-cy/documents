# Chapter I: Volunteer Membership {#volunteer-membership}

## Article 1: Active Members {#active-members}

* 1.1. Registration

    * 1.1.1. All volunteers upon officially entering the organisation must register in the Galaxy Portal as imposed by
      ESN AISBL.
    * 1.1.2. All volunteers are required to renew their registration at the beginning of every semester to maintain
      active status.
    * 1.1.3. Failure to register may result in the suspension of membership.

* 1.2. Rights of Volunteers: The Statutory rights of the volunteers are established in the Statutes of the organisation.
  The following are the additional rights established by custom and practice:

    * 1.2.1. Access to specific benefits and partnerships established each year for all volunteers.
    * 1.2.2. A discount on the price of an ESNcard, which is voted on in a General Assembly.
    * 1.2.3. Usage of the tools, services, and resources of ESN Cyprus and ESN AISBL;
    * 1.2.4. Participation in ESN Cyprus events, programs, and projects, within the limits of available places and
      resources and in accordance with selection procedures.
    * 1.2.5. Appropriate consultation by National Board representatives on matters concerning the National and
      International level of operations of the organisation.
    * 1.2.6. The right to audit a Board Member, in accordance with the procedures outlined in Article 13.3 of the
      Statutes.

* 1.3. Obligations of Volunteers: The Statutory obligations of the volunteers are established in the Statutes of the
  organisation. The following are the additional obligations established by custom and practice:

    * 1.3.1. No volunteer shall profit financially from the section or its activities.
    * 1.3.2. Volunteers must not derive any personal financial gain from their position within the section.
    * 1.3.3. If a volunteer applies for and is accepted into a position at ESN International, they must inform the Board
      of ESN Cyprus within 7 days of their acceptance.
    * 1.3.4. Payment of participation fees for trips and events where applicable, the section shall not receive any
      profit from the participation fee of Volunteers.
    * 1.3.5. Comply with all official documents of ESN Cyprus and ESN AISBL, and to act in accordance with ESN's
      mission, vision, objectives, and values.
    * 1.3.6. Respect the decisions of the Board and the General Assembly and to respond to their requests within the
      deadlines established by them.
    * 1.3.7. Fulfill the responsibilities assigned to them within ESN Cyprus in accordance with the established
      timelines.
    * 1.3.8. Not to cause any damage to the association, including actions that could harm its image.

## Article 2: Alumni Members {#alumni-members}

* 2.1. Alumnus status is granted to former active members who have honourably concluded their service to the network.

* 2.2. Rights of Alumni

    * 2.2.1. Access to an ESNcard discount similar to Active Members.
    * 2.2.2. Participation as observers in General Assembly meetings.
    * 2.2.3. The right to participate in larger local and international events, subject to capacity limitations and
      priority given to active volunteers and Erasmus students.

* 2.3. Obligations of Alumni

    * 2.3.1. Alumni must adhere to the Code of Conduct and uphold the image of ESN Cyprus in the organisation's
      activities even after their active term has ended.
    * 2.3.2. To serve as a mentoring and support resource for active ESN Cyprus members.
    * 2.3.3. To provide expertise for various activities or in particularly important situations.
