# Chapter V: Administration {#administration}

## Article 13: Consultation of ESN Cyprus Volunteers {#consultation-of-esn-cyprus-volunteers}

* 13.1. The Board is obligated to obtain the volunteer's opinion on National or International matters. The methods of
  acquiring the volunteers' opinion may include:

    * 13.1. an online form
    * 13.2. discussions during Section Meetings
    * 13.3. meetings with the National Representative (NR)

* 13.2. The opinion of ESN Cyprus Volunteers shall be consultative in nature. While the final decision rests with the
  Board, the Board is required to give due and serious consideration to ESN Cyprus's expressed position. The Board shall
  communicate its reasoning when acting contrary to the expressed opinion of the volunteers.

## Article 14: Expansion and Candidate Sections {#expansion-and-candidate-sections}

* 14.1. The following structures are eligible to obtain Candidate Section status:

    * 14.1.1. An informal group, hereinafter referred to as the *Initiative Group*, organised by students, or an already
      existing association of one or more higher education institutions in Cyprus, which benefits from demonstrable
      support from such institution (s), pursues the mission and objectives of ESN Cyprus, and conducts activities
      compatible with those of ESN Cyprus, may initiate the process of establishing an ESN Section in Cyprus.

* 14.2. Admission process: The Initiative Group shall be admitted as Candidate Section by the General Assembly according
  to the following procedure:

    * 14.2.1. The applicant organisation submits an application to the National Board. The National Board has the right
      to reject the application but must report that to the General Assembly, stating the reasons for rejection.
    * 14.2.2. The Initiative Group must present itself at the first or second General Assembly after the National Board
      forwarded the application to the members of ESN Cyprus.
    * 14.2.3. At the General Assembly meeting where the status is voted upon, the Initiative Group, represented by at
      least one member, must present its activity and respond to any requests for additional information.
    * 14.2.4. The General Assembly votes, with a ⅔ majority of the votes cast of the ESN Cyprus members having the right
      to vote, to give the applicant organisation the status of Candidate Section during a General Assembly meeting in
      accordance with national statutes or established procedures.
    * 14.2.5. Within five days of the General Assembly vote, the National Board shall communicate the decision to the
      Initiative Group.
    * 14.2.6. In the event of an unfavorable decision, the Initiative Group may restart the procedure after 30 days from
      receiving the decision.

* 14.3. Rights of a Candidate Section:

    * 14.3.1. Choose its name provided that:
        * 14.3.1.1. The element ESN is not included, unless followed or preceded by the words “Candidate Section”
    * 14.3.2. The name of the state or references to religious or political ideologies is not included unless they are a
      part of the name of the city or Higher Education Institution where the Candidate Section operates.
    * 14.3.2. Attend section meetings and General Assemblies.
    * 14.3.3. Attend international events and meetings of ESN International upon invitation by theInternational Board.

* 14.4. Obligations of a Candidate Section:

    * 14.4.1. To comply with these Statutes, the Internal Rules, the Official Documents and other governing rules of ESN
      Cyprus and ESN International;
    * 14.4.2. To bare the name and the abbreviation of the ESN Cyprus, followed or preceded by the words “Candidate
      Section” next to their name or the abbreviation of their name;
    * 14.4.3. To keep in contact and cooperation with ESN Cyprus and ESN International in order to align its activity
      with the Aims and Object of the ESN and receive guidance in that regard during the admission procedure.

* 14.5. In case a Candidate Section is considered to be added or has been added, this must be communicated by the
  National Board with ESN International and the Membership Team, in order to follow the prescribed process at an
  international level.

## Article 15: Organisation of International Events {#organisation-of-international-events}

* 15.1. A proposal for an international project may be initiated by a group of volunteer members and/or alumni of ESN
  Cyprus by completing the standard international project forms and submitting them to ESN AISBL.

* 15.2. The initiative group is required to notify the National Board and the members of the General Assembly of their
  intention to apply at least 7 days prior to the submission deadline to ESN AISBL.

* 15.3. No later than 7 calendar days before the deadline, the initiative group must submit to the National Board and
  the ESN Cyprus General Assembly the standard application forms (narrative application, budget, and any additional
  documents, where applicable).

* 15.4. The National Board has the right, within a maximum of 3 calendar days, to halt the initiative if irregularities
  are identified that may cause prejudice to ESN Cyprus. In such cases, the ESN Cyprus National Board shall provide
  support to the group in order to remedy the application.

* 15.5. If no improvements are made that resolve the identified issues, the ESN Cyprus National Board may prohibit the
  group from continuing the initiative and may inform ESN AISBL of the situation.

## Article 16: Transition of Positions {#transition-of-positions}

* 16.1. All outgoing position holders are obliged to ensure a complete, accurate, and good-faith transition to their
  successors. This includes the timely transfer of information, documents, access credentials, financial records,
  ongoing project details, and any other materials necessary for the effective continuation of the role.

* 16.2. The handover must be conducted in a transparent and responsible manner, providing correct and sufficient
  information to allow the incoming position holder to assume their duties without disruption.
