# Chapter IV: Functional Operations {#functional-operations}

## Article 8: Third-Party Events and Promotion {#third-party-events-and-promotion}

* 8.1. The promotion of third-party events are subject to the discretion of the Board.

* 8.2. Criteria for Approval

    * 8.2.1. The Board must retain control over the ESN brand within the event.
    * 8.2.2. Evaluation of potential financial gain for ESN Cyprus.
    * 8.2.3. Quality assurance of the event.

* 8.3. Categories of Events

    * 8.3.1. Prohibited: Events where a member receives a personal benefit or is a related party to the organiser that
      receives a financial benefit.
    * 8.3.2. Permitted: Cultural events that align with ESN values.
    * 8.3.3. Discretionary: Other events are subject to Board approval based on the criteria in Article 8.2.

## Article 9: Financial Procedures {#financial-procedures}

* 9.1. Each Treasurer shall codify their process for reimbursements. Valid receipts are mandatory for all claims.

* 9.2. Events must be budgeted to ensure the lowest possible price for participants while ensuring the financial
  stability of ESN Cyprus.

* 9.3. The Treasurer holds final authority over ticket pricing on financial grounds, after consultation with the OC and
  the Vice President for Coordination.

* 9.4. For ticketed events, the OCs should follow these steps:
    * 9.4.1. First, develop the event concept, identify venues, and prepare a preliminary cost structure.
    * 9.4.2. The proposal shall then be reviewed with the Vice President for Coordination to assess the quality and
      feasibility of the event.
    * 9.4.3. The OC shall consult the Treasurer regarding ticket pricing and financial planning.

## Article 10: Organising Committees (OCs) and Event Governance {#organising-committees-and-event-governance}

* 10.1. ESN Cyprus organises events on a regular basis through dedicated OCs. All events must be planned and executed in
  alignment with the values, mission, and operational guidelines of ESN AISBL.

* 10.2. Each OC shall appoint a Head of OC. The Head of OC is responsible for the overall coordination and execution of
  the event, communication with the National Board and Board Supporters, and ensuring that the event respects principles
  of safety, inclusivity, and sustainability.

* 10.3. Members of an OC are expected to actively contribute to the preparation and delivery of the event. In cases of
  inactivity, a member may be removed from the OC following consultation with the Vice President for Coordination. Such
  decisions shall be taken in good faith and in the interest of the effective functioning of the event.

* 10.4. The Treasurer holds final authority over ticket pricing on financial grounds, after consultation with the OC and
  the Vice President for Coordination.

* 10.5. The Vice President for Coordination shall appoint the head of trips that have a duration of more than 12 hours.

## Article 11: Teams {#teams}

* 11.1. Each National Board Member may establish a support team to assist with operations and projects within their
  portfolio.

* 11.2. The Teams shall operate in alignment with the mission, principles, and values of ESN Cyprus and ESN AISBL.

* 11.3. Recruitment of volunteers for such teams shall take place at least once per academic semester through an open
  and transparent process.

* 11.4. The removal of a member of the Team may take place due to inactivity or behaviour that materially disrupts the
  functioning of the team, only after the consultation with the Vice President for Administration.

## Article 12: Legal and Compliance Matters {#legal-and-compliance-matters}

* 12.1. External Agreements

    * 12.1.1. Any partnership, sponsorship, or offer provided by a venue or third party in relation to an event
      organised by ESN Cyprus shall be documented in writing. Whenever possible, the terms shall be formalised through a
      written contract.
    * 12.1.2. Where a formal contract is not applicable, the agreement must be clearly recorded through official written
      communication, such as email or other traceable written messages, ensuring transparency and accountability for all
      parties involved.

* 12.2. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

    * 12.2.1. Volunteers of ESN Cyprus shall respect and protect personal data in compliance with applicable data
      protection legislation, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
    * 12.2.2. In the course of organising events, communication activities, and internal operations, volunteers may
      collect or process personal data such as names, contact information, participation records, photographs, video
      recordings, and other information necessary for the functioning of ESN Cyprus.
    * 12.2.3. Any photographs or video material captured during events may be used for documentation, reporting,
      promotional, and communication purposes, including publication on digital platforms and social media, provided
      that such use is respectful, proportionate, and aligned with the non-profit mission of ESN Cyprus.
    * 12.2.4. Volunteers shall collect only data necessary for legitimate organisational purposes, ensure that such data
      is stored securely, limit access to authorized persons, and avoid retaining personal data longer than required.
    * 12.2.5. Volunteers must respect the rights of participants and members to be informed about how their data is
      used, and to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data where applicable, in accordance with GDPR
      provisions.
    * 12.2.6. Volunteers shall take reasonable technical and organisational measures to prevent unauthorised access,
      misuse, loss, or disclosure of personal data.
