# Chapter II: General Assembly Framework {#general-assembly-framework}

## Article 3: Procedures {#procedures}

* 3.1. Voting

    * 3.1.1. Procedures for the validation of votes and the specific presentation of votes are defined in the Statute.
    * 3.1.2. Further explanation of these procedures shall be provided by the Chairing Team at the start of every GA.

* 3.2. Chairing Team

    * 3.2.1. The Chairing Team is responsible for safeguarding the procedural integrity of the General Assembly and
      ensuring that all proceedings are conducted in compliance with applicable law, the Statutes, ESN AISBL provisions,
      and the principles of democracy, transparency, and fairness.
    * 3.2.2. The Chairing Team shall consist of three members: a Main Chair, a Vice Chair, and a Technical Chair.
        * 3.2.2.1. The Main Chair is responsible for coordinating the Chairing Team and facilitating the procedures,
          discussions, and question-and-answer sessions during the General Assembly.
        * 3.2.2.2. The Vice Chair is responsible for recording the minutes of the meeting and managing amendments to the
          agenda in accordance with established procedures.
        * 3.2.2.3. The Technical Chair is responsible for the technical execution of the General Assembly, including
          livestreaming where applicable, presentation management, and ensuring the functionality of all platforms used
          during the proceedings.
    * 3.2.3. All voting platforms used during the General Assembly must comply with GDPR requirements.
    * 3.2.4. In the case of secret ballots, identifying information, including email addresses, shall not be stored or
      retained.

## Article 4: Timeline of General Assemblies {#timeline-of-general-assemblies}

* 4.1. In order to ensure transparency, predictability, and democratic governance, the organisation shall organise
  General Assemblies according to a fixed annual framework.

    * 4.1.1. The National Assembly shall take place annually between late June and early July and shall constitute the
      primary electoral Assembly of the organisation. Its core competencies include:
        * 4.1.1.1. Election of the National Board members;
        * 4.1.1.2. Presentation by the Treasurer of the preliminary budget for the upcoming financial year;
        * 4.1.1.3. Any additional matters submitted for vote in accordance with Assembly procedures.
    * 4.1.2. The October General Assembly serves as the primary financial ratification Assembly. Its core competencies
      include:
        * 4.1.1.1. Presentation by the Treasurer of amendments to the preliminary budget and adoption of the final
          annual budget;
        * 4.1.1.2. Approval of Board Supporters appointed during the autumn period;
        * 4.1.1.3. Any additional matters submitted for vote in accordance with Assembly procedures.
    * 4.1.3. A February General Assembly may be convened where necessary to ensure continuity of governance. Its
      competencies include:
        * 4.1.1.1. Approval of Board Supporters appointed during the spring period;
        * 4.1.1.2. Any additional matters submitted for vote in accordance with Assembly procedures
    * 4.1.4. The National Board and members may propose additional subjects for deliberation and voting at any General
      Assembly. Such proposals must be communicated to members in advance in accordance with established notice
      procedures.
