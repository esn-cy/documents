# Chapter VI: Transitional Clauses {#transitional-clauses}

## Article 17: Amendment of Standing Orders {#amendment-of-standing-orders}

* 17.1. Amendments to the Standing Orders may be proposed by the National Board or by at least one-third (1/3) of the
  officially registered active members.

* 17.2. Proposed amendments must be submitted in writing to the National Board and circulated to all members no later
  than fourteen (14) calendar days before the General Assembly at which they will be discussed.

* 17.3. The notice circulated to members must include the full text of the proposed amendment and a brief explanation of
  its purpose.

* 17.4. Amendments to the Standing Orders shall be adopted by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the votes cast at a duly
  convened General Assembly, unless a higher threshold is required by the Statutes.

* 17.5. Amendments shall enter into force immediately after adoption unless the General Assembly decides otherwise.

## Article 18: Transitional Period {#transitional-period}

* 18.1. Upon adoption by the General Assembly, these Standing Orders shall enter into force on 1 November 2026.
